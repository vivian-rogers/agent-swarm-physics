## Question
Can the swarm's collective co-movement be split into a field part (all agents respond within one call of an input) and a coupling part (agents respond over one or more call cycles), using impulse responses and lag structure?

**Vivian's scope (2026-10-04):** think in transfer functions and lag. Estimate impulse responses and phase from inputs (human messages, nudges, kickoffs, platform events) and between agents; a field has zero relative lag, a coupling a lag quantized in call cycles. Future variants: HH185–HH190.
