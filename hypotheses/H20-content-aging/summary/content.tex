\hwhat{We measure the two-time correlation $C(t_w+\tau,t_w)$ of each agent's day-mean content embedding ($t_w$: active days since kickoff) in 29 non-holdout goal periods. We test the aging slope $A$ at fixed lag ($t_w\ge2$) against a stationary swarm model. The pre-registered isotropic null is 2--3$\times$ too narrow, because content fluctuates in 5--12 of 32 dimensions; we use a null with the fitted shape. \textbf{No aging:} the random-effects $A$ over long and medium goals is $+0.021\pm0.021$. \#38 shows a kickoff relaxation: lag-1 $C$ rises 0.66$\to$0.87 in four days, then stays flat. In \#51, the test rejects $\mu=0.5$ aging. \textbf{Round 1b} adds a second embedding model, dedupe, style residualization and shared goal vectors. Every verdict holds in all 7 input configurations. Style residualization halves $A$ and removes the weak late slowing in \#51. Newcomers relax on their own clock without aging. Drive withdrawal and \#27's rivalry-to-collaboration turn leave the correlation intact. The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Glassy aging (01) of vector spins (11). The spin $\bar x_{it}$ is the day state of agent $i$: the mean of its unit-normalized, regime-whitened ($n=32$) statement embeddings (own chat and plans). $v_{it}$ is its sampling noise. $q$: plateau (style and goal content). $c_0$: short-lag ceiling. $\tau_0$: relaxation time. $\mu$: aging exponent of the Box-Cox clock $s_\mu$ ($\mu=0$ stationary, $\mu=1$ simple aging). Rival: a kickoff transient that decays in $\tau_q$ days. $n_{\rm eff}$: dimension of the day-to-day fluctuations.}
\hmath{\vspace{-8pt}
\begin{gather*}
C(t,t')=\frac{\sum_i \bar x_{it}\cdot\bar x_{it'}}{\sqrt{\sum_i S_{it}\,\sum_i S_{it'}}},\quad S_{it}=|\bar x_{it}|^2-v_{it}\\
C(t_w+\tau,t_w)=q+(c_0-q)\,e^{-\Delta s/\tau_0}\\
\Delta s=s_\mu(t_w+\tau)-s_\mu(t_w),\quad s_\mu(t)=\tfrac{t^{1-\mu}-1}{1-\mu}\\
A=\partial C/\partial\ln t_w\,\big|_{\tau},\qquad \mathrm{Var}\,C(t,t')\propto 1/n_{\rm eff}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-6pt}}

\htelos{Nothing changes for an operator who fears that a long-running swarm ``rigidifies''. At day resolution, content turnover does not slow from week one to week three. Weak aging is not excluded. After a large kickoff, allow about four active days for content to settle (seen once, \#38). For an analyst: test embedding similarity on swarm logs against a null with the measured low-dimensional fluctuation shape. An isotropic null is 2--3$\times$ overconfident. As physics, aging finds nothing to grip: daily re-prompting with a fixed goal acts as a strong pinning field.}
\hbottom{Swarm content does not age at day resolution (both embedding models): it settles for days after a kickoff, then decorrelates at an age-independent rate.}
