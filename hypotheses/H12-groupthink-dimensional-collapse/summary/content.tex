\htelos{The portable results concern what is \emph{not} collective. The activity market mode was mostly a data bug plus the daily schedule. Under a calibrated null, agents are busy or idle together beyond chance in only 7/24 units. What agents talk about does co-move, in content and talk modes. A debate motion, a strong uniform field, narrows content by 25\%, and a contested vote narrows it too. Goal kickoffs do not. The cheapest useful monitor is self-restatement: its rate tracks low PR at $\rho=-0.75$. It flags agents that rephrase themselves, the village's real low-dimensional states. The groupthink alarm stays unvalidated: cross-agent echo never exceeds a few percent.}

\hbottom{The activity market mode is mostly artifact; talk and content modes are real, a strong field compresses content, and low-PR weeks are self-restatement.}

\hcaveats{Two causes explain the collapse of the activity mode: the bug's day-level common loss and the scheduler's start and stop. H50 finds the scheduler effect independently from call windows. Within-block co-activation rises on the fixed table. PR measures what agents write, including format: debate turns are structured speeches, and the \#12 off window is short. P7's direction depends on the embedding model and on restatement removal. Whitening is per regime, so PR levels compare only within a regime. In 3-day units the cross-day edge gives $\sim$14\% false positives. The holdout is untouched. Amendment 2's C1--C3 (activity mode) and C5 (day-1 expansion) look unlikely to pass as frozen.}
