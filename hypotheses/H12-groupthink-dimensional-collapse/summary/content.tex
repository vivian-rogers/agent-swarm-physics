PR: participation ratio of the content covariance $\Sigma$ ($d=32$), bias-corrected via $W=\nu S$, $\nu=n-1$.}

C_{ij}=\langle s_i s_j\rangle_t,\;\; \lambda_+=\big(1+\sqrt{N/T}\big)^2,\;\; k=\#\{\lambda_k>\lambda_+\}\\

\end{gather*}\vspace{-10pt}}

\htelos{The portable results are about what is \emph{not} collective. The activity ``market mode'' was mostly a data bug plus the daily schedule: under a calibrated null agents are not busy or idle together beyond chance in most weeks. What agents talk about does co-move (content and talk modes), and a strong uniform field (a debate motion) or a contested vote measurably narrows it, while goal kickoffs widen it. The cheapest useful monitor remains self-restatement: it flags agents stuck rephrasing themselves, the village's real low-dimensional states. The groupthink alarm stays unvalidated: cross-agent echo never exceeded a few percent.}

\hbottom{On corrected data the activity market mode is mostly artifact; talk and content modes are real, a strong field or a contested vote compresses content, kickoffs do not, and low-dimensional weeks are agents restating themselves.}
