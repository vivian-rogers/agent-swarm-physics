\hwhat{24 non-holdout units (goal periods split at step changes), two arms. \textbf{Spectrum:} the agent$\times$agent correlation matrices of activity, talk and chat content each have exactly one eigenvalue above the cross-day surrogate edge (22/24, 20/24, 24/24 units), never more than two. It is uniform across agents ($\mathrm{VR}/\lambda_1$ median 0.97): the Curie--Weiss ``market mode'', ranking like H02's $\beta J_0$ ($\rho=0.73$). But it is partly synchronized lulls: it survives dropping minutes with $\le 1$ active agent in only 14/24 units. \textbf{Dimensionality failed, in direction:} the participation ratio of chat did not collapse at 20 kickoffs (median $+7\%$, $p=0.86$ vs placebos; $+44\%$ in regime III); day 1 is more diverse than later days (13/16, $p=0.02$); free weeks are not more diverse than shared ones ($p=0.58$). The lowest-PR weeks (\#38--\#40) are agents repeating their own text (16--60\% self near-copies), not copying each other. Holdout script frozen, not run.}

\hmodel{Models 01 (inverse Ising), 11 (vector spins), random-matrix theory. $s_i(t)$: agent $i$'s $\pm1$ activity or talk spin per minute, or whitened content vector per 30-min window ($N$ agents, $T$ samples). $C$: equal-time correlation matrix, eigenvalues $\lambda_k$; $\lambda_+$: noise edge (used: cross-day surrogates); $u=\mathbf 1/\sqrt N$; $\bar\rho$: mean pair correlation. PR: participation ratio of the content covariance $\Sigma$ ($d=32$), bias-corrected via $W=\nu S$, $\nu=n-1$.}

\hmath{\vspace{-8pt}\begin{gather*}
C_{ij}=\langle s_i s_j\rangle_t,\;\; \lambda_+=\big(1+\sqrt{N/T}\big)^2,\;\; k=\#\{\lambda_k>\lambda_+\}\\

\end{gather*}\vspace{-10pt}}

\htelos{The most portable result refutes its own premise. Dimensional collapse is a bad groupthink alarm: kickoffs make agents \emph{more} diverse, and the truly low-dimensional states were single agents stuck in loops, re-posting near-copies of their own messages. Anyone with an (agent, time, text) log can compute self-repetition and cross-agent echo rates in minutes, with nothing fitted; self-repetition flagged this village's worst weeks. That is a cheap stuck-agent monitor. The ``market mode'' is less useful: agents are busy or idle together, partly platform-wide lulls. The groupthink alarm stays unvalidated: echo never exceeded 8\% here.}

\hbottom{The swarm has one collective mode and does not collapse at kickoffs; its low-dimensional episodes are agents looping on themselves, which a cheap self-repetition rate detects.}
