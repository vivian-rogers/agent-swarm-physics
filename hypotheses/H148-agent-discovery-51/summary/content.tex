\hwhat{The claim: an autonomous search can find which systems in \#51 behave as individuals, at every scale and nested, with no candidate groups named in advance. A system is a set of atoms (agents, repos, rooms, H145 meaning elements). It is an individual when its own state predicts its future beyond everything outside it, on held-out days, and that holds when the search runs on odd days and is scored on even days and the reverse. \textbf{Result:} at 30-min resolution the search finds no system larger than one atom that holds out of sample, at the agent level or the element level. Its in-sample maxima are as many on time-rotated data. Agents' self-information lives at the day scale: a within-day shuffle of an agent's own states removes it for 26 of 27 agents.}

\hmodel{Information dynamics (model 12), Krakauer individuality and boundary expansion. Atom $a$ joins system $X$ when it carries information about $X$'s future while it sits in the environment, and $X$ about $a$'s: the drop in non-closure $n_C$ on both sides. Each term is a held-out (leave-one-day-out) log-loss difference given the environment $E$ (phase, exogenous input incl.\ relayed human input, rest-of-village presence). The null shuffles $a$'s days within blocks of six same-parity days; it keeps $a$'s within-day dynamics and phase.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
\tau(a,X)=I(x';a\mid x,E)+I(a';x\mid a,E)\\
z_\tau=(\tau-\langle\tau_{\rm perm}\rangle)/\sigma_{\rm perm},\quad \text{add if } z_\tau\ge3
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{Do not expect 30-minute activity logs to reveal multi-agent individuals in a village like \#51. An agent's next state is predicted by which project it holds that day, not by its last half hour; give each agent a day plan it can keep. To find nested individuals, log at the day scale over months, or plant interventions; the search here needs a planted pair coupling of $\rho\ge0.9$ to reach 0.65 recovery.}

\hbottom{No system larger than one atom behaves as an individual out of sample in \#51 at 30 minutes; agents are individuals only at the day scale.}

\hcaveats{The search scores 30-min transitions within days; day-scale structure is in its nulls by design. The synthetic agents had no day-level persistence, so the pre-registered single-atom null was not checked against day-scale individuality. Power is low: K3 makes P3 and P4 not identifiable, and P2 untestable. Ten amendments (A1--A10) replace the card's matched-random excess, which grew without bound in the synthetic worlds; all are dated before real data. A diagnostic read the plug-in lagged information of 12 real agent--repo pairs before the synthetic commit (disclosed). The reserved tail 51m is not used.}
