\hmodel{Vector spins on two sublattices (11), with $q=2$ Potts (10) and mean field (01). Spin $\mathbf s_i$: the masked, whitened ($n=32$), agent-centred chat mean of debater $i$ in a debate. Sublattice $A$ = Government ($\varepsilon_i=+1$), $B$ = Opposition. Fields: motion topic $h_u\hat g$ (uniform), assigned sides $h_s\hat a$ (staggered), family $\mathbf h_i$, verdict $h_v$ toward winner $W$. Couplings: $k_{\rm in}$ (team, ferromagnetic), $k_{\rm out}$ (cross-team, antiferromagnetic). $\rho$: correlation of sublattice fluctuations.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
H=-\tfrac{k_{\rm in}}{N}\textstyle\sum_{i\sim j}\mathbf s_i\!\cdot\!\mathbf s_j+\tfrac{k_{\rm out}}{N}\sum_{A\times B}\mathbf s_i\!\cdot\!\mathbf s_j\\
\textstyle-\sum_i\big(h_u\hat g+h_s\varepsilon_i\hat a+h_v\varepsilon_W\hat a+\mathbf h_i\big)\!\cdot\!\mathbf s_i\\

\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\hcaveats{Round 1b's DQ2 stance is zero-shot (pair-level ``opposes'' precision 0.08). Round 1c's validated flag is better (precision 0.61--0.67) but still one labeller and 33 flags, and teammates' 0/60 leaves the log-odds effect unbounded. The agent-field null assumes replies are independent given agents. Stance measures agreement of positions, so the assigned staggered field alone predicts the order. A coupling beyond the assignment is not shown: content fluctuations stay positively correlated. The sample is seven agents over four days in regime I. \#26 has 9 voters with near-identical ballots (low power), and \#34 is untouched.}
