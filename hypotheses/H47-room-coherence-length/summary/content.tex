\hwhat{The degrees of freedom are the agents' 32-d content vectors in 30-min windows. The test compares per-pair content correlation within rooms ($\rho_w$) and across rooms ($\rho_c$) on 9 multi-room periods, against room-relabel nulls. Three native tests follow: the NE42 merge and split, \#41's identical instructions and \#51's \#focus room. Synthetic worlds come first. Rooms bound coherence: $C_B=\rho_c/\rho_w\le0.3$ in 6/9 periods (median 0.23). The merge of \#best and \#rest joins their content fluctuations, and the split restores the boundary (DiD 1.0, $p$ 0.001). Inside rooms, the correlation sits in pairs that talk. Post hoc, the boundary sharpness tracks topic separation. A per-room detector gives no gain (2 clean events). No room leads: both rooms switch within minutes. \textbf{Mixed.} The holdout script exists and is not run.}

\hmodel{Soft-spin O($n$) vector field (model 11) on a room graph. The spin $s_i$ is an agent's 32-d whitened statement mean per 30-min window, minus its unit mean and static goal directions. Distance is categorical: same room, different room and, inside a room, high and low mention tiers. Fields are global (goal, schedule) or room-specific (instructions, tasks). The coupling runs through the room's chat. $S_i$ is the split-half signal variance.}

\htelos{To keep a group's content independent, give it its own room and a different task. Two rooms with the same task share about half of each room's content fluctuation. Rooms with different tasks share almost none. A merge joins coherence within days, and a split restores the boundary at once (NE42). Inside a 25-agent room, coherence sits in conversation pairs, so a monitor must track pairs, not the room mean. Goal changes reach every room within minutes, so no leader room exists to watch. A per-room topic alarm adds nothing to H36's swarm alarm. $C_B$ cannot separate room coupling from room-specific task fields, so it gives no handle for steering through coupling.}

\hbottom{Rooms bound coherence when they do different things, and the bound moves with the chat channel; inside rooms coherence follows conversation, and no room leads.}

\hcaveats{$C_B$ is the global share of a room's fluctuation. Room coupling and room-specific task fields both lower it, and the synthetic worlds show that $C_B$ cannot separate them.
