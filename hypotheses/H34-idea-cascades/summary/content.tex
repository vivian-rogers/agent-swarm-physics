\hwhat{We hashed novel terms, URLs, numbers and names in 32 non-holdout goal periods (132k ideas). Each agent's first use was parented to the latest visible earlier use, giving exposure trees. Ideas spread subcritically everywhere: $\hat R=0.06$--$0.39$, all CIs below 1. A new idea reaches a second agent 6--24\% of the time. Round 1b (context-ledger visibility): adoption is time-locked to recent visible uses beyond a common field in 32/32 periods (round 1: 27/32; regime I was mismeasured). But uses not yet read predict adoption at about half that strength: part of the lock is convergence, not copying. Pure $s^{-3/2}$ is rejected in all 32; tails are heavier than one branching law. Leaders are not idea sources. Failed: activity loop gains do not predict spread; the pre-registered forecast was overconfident. Holdout not run.}

\hmodel{Contagion (model 03) as a Galton--Watson cascade (09) in a room of $N$ agents. Adopters have negative-binomial offspring with mean $R$ (branching ratio) and dispersion $k$. $s$: tree size. $s_c$: cutoff. $h_k$: adoption hazard per talk turn with $k$ recent visible sources. $R_c$: contagion share of $\hat R$. $\eta$: daily drift of $R$ in the forecast rule.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
\hat R=\frac{\#\,\text{first uses with an agent parent}}{\#\,\text{agent first uses}},\quad
R_c=\hat R\,\bigl(1-h_0/h_{\ge1}\bigr)\\
P(s\mid R,k)=\frac{\Gamma(ks+s-1)}{\Gamma(ks)\Gamma(s+1)}\frac{(R/k)^{s-1}}{(1+R/k)^{ks+s-1}}\sim s^{-3/2}e^{-s/s_c}\\
s_c^{-1}=R-1-\ln R,\qquad R_d=\hat R\,e^{\eta},\ \ \eta\sim\mathcal N(0,0.25^2)
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{The usable output is a two-number forecast. Measure $\hat R$ (first uses with a visible parent, over all first uses) on the last two days of logs. At the village's $\hat R\approx0.2$, expect 83\% of new ideas or claims to stay with their author and about 1 in 150 to reach five agents. With day drift added, this post-hoc rule reaches 70--84\% coverage at 90\% nominal; it is frozen for the holdout. The negative lessons matter more. Activity criticality (Hawkes $n$) says nothing about idea spread. In a shared room, ``who saw it first'' cannot separate copying from independent invention: compare read with not-yet-read uses at the same lag. A power-law exponent is just $\hat R$ in disguise. Nothing is near runaway: misinformation here dies locally unless it is an artifact everyone must touch.}

\hbottom{Idea cascades in the village are strongly subcritical ($\hat R\approx0.2$), exposure-driven and heterogeneous rather than power-law. Branching ratio, not exponent or activity loop gain, is the number that forecasts how far an idea spreads.}

\hcaveats{``Ideas'' are hashed marker rules, not semantic units, and $\hat R$ depends on the rule. Field vs contagion rests on $\mathrm{HR}_{10}$: a bursty common trigger (a web page or human prompt reaching several agents at once) would mimic it, and copying from tools or the web is invisible. Round 1's call-start visibility undercounted regime-I exposure; 1b uses the ledger. Unread uses are more recent than read ones, so the convergence share is an upper bound. Novelty is relative to non-holdout text only. The H25 content-dial link (partial $\rho=0.41$ given room size; daily $\rho=0.28$) is post hoc and shares its source text with $\hat R$. Periods with rooms of 4 cannot show tails.}
