\hmodel{Contagion (model 03) runs as a Galton--Watson cascade (model 09) in a room of $N$ agents with depletion (FN-GW). The degree of freedom is an agent's first use of an idea. Each idea $i$ has its own ratio $R_{0,i}$, gamma-distributed with mean $\mu$, shape $\alpha$ (CV$^2=1/\alpha$). $P_{\rm FN}(s\mid R_0)$ is the exact size law. $\rho_{\rm off}$: non-root over root offspring. $R_{\rm src}$: a group's offspring per first use; $\beta$ scales the room-size rule.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
\hat R=\frac{\#\,\text{first uses with an agent parent}}{\#\,\text{agent first uses}},\qquad R_{0,i}\sim\Gamma\!\left(\alpha,\ \mu/\alpha\right)\\
P(\{s_{ij}\}_j)=\int\! dR_0\ \Gamma(R_0;\alpha,\mu/\alpha)\prod_j P_{\rm FN}(s_{ij}\mid R_0)\\
\Delta\ln R_{\rm src}=\beta\cdot0.451\,\ln\frac{N_a-1}{N_b-1}+\text{boundary effect}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}
