\hwhat{HH338 says that a spontaneously broken continuous symmetry has a soft direction. The room content difference of identical-kickoff weeks should then wander from day to day (a Goldstone mode), while room-specific instructions pin it. H108 measures the day-to-day persistence of the \#best/\#rest content direction in 8 non-holdout periods, corrected for sampling noise by joint relabels. \textbf{The pre-registered contrast passes, but it is fragile.} Directions persist inside a week (cosine 0.69--0.95 in 6/8 periods). The fielded weeks are more persistent (pooled 0.93 vs 0.70; ratio of decorrelation rates 4.7). The contrast rests on the one long fielded week, \#38, and it is confounded with split size. The fork week does not pin.}

\hmodel{Model 11 (soft-spin O($n$), $n=32$). The room order parameter $\mathbf{m}(d)=\boldsymbol{\Delta}(d)$ is the daily \#best $-$ \#rest content difference (style-residualized, day-centred, agent constants removed). Without a field its direction $\hat{\mathbf{m}}$ diffuses on the sphere with $D\propto1/(N_\mathrm{eff}|\mathbf{m}|^2)$; a field $\mathbf{h}$ adds a restoring torque, so the angle is an Ornstein--Uhlenbeck process with a plateau. $C$ and $E$ are excess cross-products over joint relabels; $P(\ell)$ estimates the lag-$\ell$ cosine.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
d\hat{\mathbf{m}}=-\Gamma\chi(\mathbf{h}\!\cdot\!\hat{\mathbf{m}}_\perp)\,dt+\sqrt{2D}\,d\boldsymbol{\xi}_\perp,\quad D\simeq\sigma^2/(2N_\mathrm{eff}|\mathbf{m}|^2)\\
P(\ell)=\frac{\sum_d C(d,d{+}\ell)}{\sqrt{\sum_d E(d)\sum_d E(d{+}\ell)}},\quad D_\theta=-\ln P(1)\\
R_D=D_\theta^{\mathrm{identical}}/D_\theta^{\mathrm{fielded}}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator, the useful fact is modest: inside a week the content difference between rooms keeps most of its direction (cosine about 0.7--0.9 a day), and one set of different room instructions held it nearly fixed for three weeks. Whether that is the instruction pinning the direction or simply a bigger split drifting more slowly (Goldstone predicts both) is not decided by seven periods. A work assignment alone (the forks) did not hold the content direction. Treat this as a description of room dynamics, not a lever.}

\hbottom{The room content direction persists from day to day inside a week and persists most under room-specific instructions (pooled cosine 0.93 vs 0.70), but the contrast rests on one long week and cannot yet be told apart from slower drift of a larger split.}
