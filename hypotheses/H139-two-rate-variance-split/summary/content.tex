\hwhat{H130 found that in \#51 a read kicks the reader's content ($J_K=0.044$) and the kick fades within $\approx$7 calls, while the agent's own well relaxes in $\approx$100 calls. HH382 asked whether that fast kick fixes, with no new fit, how much of an agent's content variance is fast. H139 measures the own content autocovariance at 1-call resolution in 15 regime-III units (\#37--\#42, \#51) and compares its fast amplitude with the shot-noise prediction. A synthetic check on the real skeletons decided first whether the prediction is resolvable. It is not: the predicted fast amplitude is 7 to $>$1{,}000 times below the smallest resolvable one in every unit. The verdict is inconclusive (below resolution).}

\hmodel{Two-rate Langevin relaxation (model 16) on the per-call clock. Content $x=s+k$: a slow part $s$ held by the agent's private well (rate $\gamma_s$) and a fast part $k$ made of read kicks of size $J$ that decay at $\gamma_k$. For independent kick directions, Campbell's theorem fixes the fast amplitude from the read rate $\bar r$ (DQ1 ledger), $J$ and $\gamma_k$ (H130). The own autocovariance, corrected for the common drive, then has two exponentials and a plateau.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
C(\tau)=A_k(1-\gamma_k)^\tau+A_s(1-\gamma_s)^\tau+B,\quad \tau\ge1\ \text{call}\\
A_k^{\rm pred}=\bar r\,J^2/[1-(1-\gamma_k)^2],\qquad Q_k=\hat A_k/A_k^{\rm pred}\\
\text{S1: } A_{\min}>2A_k^{\rm pred}\ \Rightarrow\ \text{P1 untestable}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator, the read channel is not visible in an agent's overall content fluctuations: at about one read per call, H130's kick predicts a fast share of $\approx$2.5\% of the dynamic content variance (median), well inside the noise of $\sim$10$^3$--10$^4$ statement pairs per unit. To see what reading does to an agent, measure the response along the sender's direction (H130's dose design), not the agent's total variance.}

\hcaveats{The synthetic noise level was higher than the real one; rescaling to the real bootstrap SE (post hoc) still leaves $A_{\min}/A_k^{\rm pred}\ge6.8$. The drive correction subtracts conversational co-movement at short wall lags, and the lag-1 covariance sits below the lag-2 covariance in 14/15 units, so the constrained $\hat A_k$ is negative (post hoc). $\gamma_{\rm kick}$ was not re-estimated in the shared weeks. Agent-level slow shares are unstable.}
