\hwhat{HH66 says the \#best/\#rest content difference has two parts: a response to the operator's room field (explicit symmetry breaking) and a spontaneous divergence that stays with the room when members swap. H100 decomposes the room separation in 8 non-holdout two-room periods (\#35--\#44) into composition, field and spontaneous shares. It also follows 5 operator moves. \textbf{The difference is endogenous.} After the members' own constants and the measured field directions are removed, the rooms still differ beyond random groupings in 5/7 regime-III periods ($f_\mathrm{spont}$ 0.58--0.91). Composition is small. The kickoff-text direction carries a chance share, although the two fielded weeks separate most. The divergence does not survive the next goal. One mover adopts its new room within a day, and one keeps its old position all week. The holdout (NE19) is not run.}

\hmodel{Model 11 (soft-spin O($n$), $n=32$ whitened dimensions) with the room as a Potts domain index (model 10). The spin $\mathbf{x}_{i,d}$ is agent $i$'s style-residualized content on day $d$. $\mathbf{g}_d$ is the day field (goal, schedule), $\mathbf{a}_i$ the agent constant, $\mathbf{h}_r$ the operator room field (room kickoff, operator messages) with susceptibility $\chi$, and $\mathbf{m}_r$ the spontaneous room magnetization. $S$ is the split-half room separation and $Q$ its excess over random partitions. The mover index $\varphi$ is $+1$ for a native of the new room.}

\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator, rooms with identical instructions still diverge ($Q_\mathrm{spont}$ 1.8--4.2 in 3/5 such weeks). Different instructions about double the separation ($Q$ 5--7), but the content does not line up with the instruction text, so a text-similarity monitor of ``is each room on its brief'' will miss it. Room identity does not carry over a goal change: regroup freely, nothing persists. A moved agent may align with its new room on day 1 or keep its old project for a week. Check its work, not its room. This is a modest design fact. It says where room differences come from, not how to steer them.}

\hbottom{The \#best/\#rest content split is mostly endogenous and regenerates at each goal; the operator's room instruction triggers a larger split but not along its own text, and composition explains little.}
