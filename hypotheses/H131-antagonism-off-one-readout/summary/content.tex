\hwhat{HH374 asks whether assigned antagonism switches off at each former rival's first call after it reads the verdict, as a weakly coupled kinetic Ising spin relaxes in one update when its field goes. H131 tests this in the ten judged debates of \#12 and the \#26 runoff with the validated stance~v2.1 conflict flag and the DQ1 read-out ledger. In \#12, 34 of 85 rival replies carry the flag while the prize is open; teammates carry 0 of 87. After each speaker reads the verdict, rivals drop to 0 of 57, and each rival's first post-read reply is already at 0 of 21. The HH's kill test cannot run: no reply falls between a verdict and its speaker's read, so read and wall-clock alignment coincide.}

\hmodel{Kinetic Ising on reply bonds (model 02) with a staggered two-camp field (model 10, $q=2$). A reply by $j$ to $i$ from $j$'s call $c$ carries a latent conflict $z$. $R_{ij}=1$ for drafted rivals, $O_j(c)=1$ while $j$ has not read the verdict, $h$ is the prize field, and $u_j,v_i$ are agent fields. The flag $y$ is $z$ seen through the labeller: recall $\rho\approx0.6$, false-positive rate $\varepsilon\approx0.005$. Remanence lets $h$ decay over $\kappa$ post-read talk calls; H131 says $\kappa\to0$.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
\mathrm{logit}\,P(z=1)=\mu+u_j+v_i+h\,R_{ij}\,O_j(c)\,e^{-(k-1)/\kappa}\\
P(y=1)=\rho\,P(z=1)+\varepsilon\,[1-P(z=1)]\\
\Delta=(\bar y_{R=1}-\bar y_{R=0})_{\rm open}-(\bar y_{R=1}-\bar y_{R=0})_{\rm read}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator, assigned antagonism is a field: settle the contest and make sure each party reads the settlement, and its conflict ends by its first reply after that. In \#12 that took a median of 93 s after the verdict, with no cool-down and no resentment. The design cannot yet say whether the read itself or simply the passage of time ends the conflict, because in scheduled chat every agent read the verdict within seconds.}
