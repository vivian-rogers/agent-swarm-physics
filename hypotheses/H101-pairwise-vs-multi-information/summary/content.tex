\hwhat{HH322 asked whether co-usage of conventions has structure beyond pairs: a ``group mind'' would leave multi-information that a pairwise maximum-entropy model cannot capture after fields are removed. The test covers 71 non-holdout units (282 days): per day, which active agents used which H34 marker, on random 6-agent subsets. The raw Schneidman ratio is $I_2/I_N=0.95$, but the synthetic shows this is near 1 even with a planted group term. After removing agent rates and a shared item-popularity field, pairs capture a median $\rho_F=0.91$ of the remaining multi-information. A higher-order remainder of 3--5\% of $I_N$ is significant in 83\% of units with $N\ge6$, and it stays inside what agent-specific responses to a shared field produce in 92\%. Known teams and rooms appear as signed pairwise couplings. The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Models 01 and 12. Sample: one marker used in agent chat on one day; spin $s_i=1$ if agent $i$ used it. Fields $h_i$ (agent-day rate), $V(K)$ (shared item popularity, $K$ = users), couplings $J_{ij}$. Models are fitted exactly on the $2^6-1$ patterns with $K\ge1$; entropies are bias-corrected by a parametric bootstrap from the fitted K-pairwise model.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
P(\mathbf s)\propto\exp\!\Big(\textstyle\sum_i h_is_i+\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}s_is_j+V(K)\Big),\quad K=\sum_i s_i\ge1\\
I_N=S_1-S_N,\qquad \rho_{raw}=\frac{S_1-S_2}{S_1-S_N},\qquad \phi=\frac{S_1-S_K}{S_1-S_N}\\
\rho_F=\frac{S_K-S_{2K}}{S_K-S_N},\qquad r_{HO}=\frac{S_{2K}-S_N}{S_1-S_N}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For a monitor: co-usage of names, numbers and coinages across an agent swarm is described by who-pairs-with-whom plus shared hot topics. Rooms and teams show up as pairwise blocks (opponents avoid each other's terms: $J=-0.60$ vs $+0.15$ within teams). Nothing here needs a group-level interaction, so a pairwise model of co-usage is a sufficient compressed description for steering or auditing. The cynical reading is that this instrument cannot see a group mind of moderate size: a heterogeneous shared field leaves the same remainder. Raw $I_2/I_N\approx1$ is not evidence of anything at these rates.}

\hcaveats{A group interaction of the planted size and a heterogeneous shared field give the same remainder, so the egregore question is open, not closed. Markers within one message are not independent, so item-level resampling understates uncertainty; ratios carry no CI in the estimates table. Subsets of 6 agents marginalize hidden agents, which creates real subset-level higher order. Units with 4 agents (most of 2025) have no power. Projects pool days, so their day field is not removed.}
