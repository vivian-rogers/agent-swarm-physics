\hwhat{The test asks whether designated leaders aggregate (high content inflow, broad replies, low outflow) rather than broadcast. Inflow $\chi$ and outflow $\kappa$ come from a read-gated linear-response fit on context-ledger reads, with an unread in-flight term for co-response. Reply rates come from DQ2. The fit runs on 46 period units and four native tests: an elected leader (\#26), rotating lead designers (\#35), an installed leader (\#44) and rotating judges (\#12). Synthetic runs came first and recalibrated the single-leader test. \textbf{No leader is a router} (0/4 natives). Lead designers and judges get +0.39 and +0.45 more replies per statement ($p\le 0.007$), and judges answer more agents. Their content inflow does not rise. The elected leader is a content \emph{source}. Across units, answering raises inflow ($\rho$ +0.29, 36/46), but attention is unrelated to content flow. \textbf{Mixed.} The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Directed linear response (model 02) and information flow (model 04). The content state $z_B$ of agent $j$'s statement is a whitened 32-d embedding with the goal and kickoff field projected out. It responds to $j$'s own past, the day $\times$ room field $f$, read human messages $H$, unread in-flight messages $U$, and the decayed read input $S_{iB}$ from each agent $i$. Inflow $\chi_j$ is the slope on all reads. Outflow $\kappa_i$ is the extra slope on $i$'s reads in other agents' statements.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
z_B=a z_P+a'e_j+b f+h H_B+\lambda U_B+\textstyle\sum_i \kappa_{i\to j} S_{iB}+\xi_B\\
S_{iB}=\frac{\sum_{A\in i,\ \mathrm{read}} w_A z_A}{1+\sum w_A},\quad w_A=e^{-\mathrm{age}/15\,\mathrm{min}}\\
\chi_j:\ \kappa_{i\to j}\equiv\chi_j\ \forall i,\qquad \rho_R=\mathrm{pct}(\chi)-\mathrm{pct}(\kappa)
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{Do not install a leader to collect or relay information. In this village the role buys attention: about 0.4 more replies per statement and, for judges, broader answering. It does not make the leader absorb more content or pass it on. An elected leader can become a content source, which an operator should treat as an agenda field on the swarm. To route information, address the agents who must act on it; a hub does not relay it. Per-agent content coefficients are noisy, and same-room messages the agent had not yet read carry 0.6 of the read response.}

\hbottom{Designated leaders are attention hubs, not content routers; the one elected leader is a content source.}
