\hwhat{HH379 asks whether each agent commits to a kickoff's named target at its own first call that reads the kickoff, plus about one call, as a zero-temperature Potts spin aligns at its first update in a strong field. The rival is a common delay after the kickoff post (R-clock). The test needs agents who were active before the kickoff but read it late. H136 counted them first, before computing any freeze time. In 17 non-reserved kickoff units (G18--G44, regimes I--III), no unit has more than 2 such delayed active readers; 5 are needed. The kill is untestable on exploration data, and the outcome analysis stopped there.}

\hmodel{Zero-temperature kinetic Potts quench with read-out updates (model 10; asynchronous updates, model 02). A field $h$ on the named target $x$ appears at the kickoff post $t_k$. Agent $i$ updates only at its own calls, and the field enters at its kickoff read-out call $t_{r,i}$ (DQ1 ledger). With $h$ above every other field, the agent freezes on $x$ $K_i$ calls after the read, with $K_i$ independent of the read delay $D_{r,i}$. R-clock instead freezes at $t_k+D_i$, truncated at the read.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
t_{f,i}=t_{r,i}+K_i\ \text{calls},\quad K_i\sim\mathrm{Geom},\quad K_i\perp D_{r,i}=t_{r,i}-t_k\\
\text{R-clock: } t_{f,i}=\max(t_k+D_i,\,t_{r,i}),\quad D_i\perp D_{r,i}\\
\text{O1: } D_{f,i}=a+b\,D_{r,i}\quad(\text{H136: } b=1;\ \text{R-clock: } b\approx0)
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator, a goal kickoff posted just before the day starts reaches every agent at boot: 96\% of 178 readers read it within 2 active minutes, with a median wall-clock delay of about 1 minute. At that spread, read-locked and clock-locked commitment predict the same timing. The question only matters for a field that arrives mid-day, while agents are busy or paused, and the exploration data have no such kickoff.}

\hbottom{In 17 day-start kickoff units, 171 of 178 kickoff readers (96\%) read the kickoff within 2 active min, at day boot, with at most 2 delayed active readers per unit, so the read-locked vs clock freeze test is untestable on exploration data.}
