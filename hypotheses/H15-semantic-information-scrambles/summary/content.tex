\hmodel{Model 04, Kolchinsky--Wolpert semantic information. The system $X$ is one agent: its memory $M$ (day-scale store) and its context window $C$ (session-scale store, filled by chat and events). $Y$ is the rest of the village. A scramble erases $M$ or $C$, or cuts $Y\to X$; $\hat p$ is the scrambled joint. $V$ is viability per agent-day (5 candidates) or per call (work). $u$ is its same-day residual, and $\delta$ is the fraction removed. $S$ is the semantic information and $\eta$ its share of $I(X;Y)$.}
