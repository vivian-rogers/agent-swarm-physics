# H15 × NE27: batch join of GPT-5, Grok 4, Claude Opus 4.1 (2025-08-18, goal #10)

**Verdict:** supported on V* (small, fragile)
**Role:** exploratory (round 1, non-holdout); the three newcomers are MN events in `../G10/`.

## Why this NE
Three agents with empty memories join at once (N 4 → 7) on the day goal #10 starts. In KW terms each is a full erasure of stored information (δ = 1). The batch also dilutes the swarm (H01 D5.2.b).

## Design
Newcomer deficit (card O3): mean u over tenure active days 1–3 minus days 6–12, net of incumbents through same-day differencing; null = incumbents' same contrast at pseudo-join days in #10. The reference window (tenure days 6–12) falls in #11–#12 (exception (c): the join is the object). Spillover (O8): incumbents' undifferenced V around the join vs. placebo days.
Confound: the goal change on the same day. Same-day differencing removes it only to the extent it hits incumbents and newcomers alike.

## Prediction
*Written 2026-10-03, before running.* Card P4 on V* (V_rel, regime I) and on V_eng: deficit ≤ −0.3 SD (with 3 events the period test has little power; the sign is what counts here). Card P10: incumbents unchanged beyond the placebo band. V_out is degenerate in 2025-08 (no write verbs), so it is not used.

- On V* (reliability, 1 − error fraction) all three newcomers sit slightly below the incumbents' own day 1–3 vs 6–12 contrast; the raw deficits are tiny (−0.04 to −0.08) and the significance comes from a tight null built from only 4 incumbents × 5 days (the small-pool problem of amendment A1 applies here). On engagement and plan coherence the newcomers are, if anything, *more* active early (novelty).
- Spillover (O8) can't be estimated: #10 has 5 active days, too few for placebo dates.
- Reading: no sign that three empty-memory agents joining at once cost the swarm anything visible at the day scale.
