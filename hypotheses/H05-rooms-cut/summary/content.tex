\hwhat{Do rooms make agents coupled blocks? Round 1 (exploratory, 99 non-holdout days, \#35--\#44) fitted 1-min talk and activity spins. Within-room talk coupling beat cross-room in 6/6 regime-III two-room windows (kinetic-Ising $J$, Fisher $p<10^{-4}$), but per period the primary $\kappa_x$ test passed only in G38, G41, G44 (mixed G37, G42; failed G39). The pooled two-way-FE effect of co-location was $\beta\approx+0.008$ ($z=2.6$). Activity spins: not robust. Entropy production stayed at the cross-day null in 15/18 windows, so it cannot be measured at this resolution. Holdout, NE15 (03-16 \#best/\#rest split): the primary pair DiD passed (talk $\kappa_x$ $-0.022$ [$-0.038,-0.008$], $p_{\rm perm}=0.02$) and $J_{\rm out}$ fell to $\approx 0$. The pooled TWFE missed ($z=1.4$), so the pre-registered verdict is \textbf{inconclusive}. Unpredicted: within-room $J_{\rm in}$ rose about $\times 6$. Caveat: the post-split window (\#35) was not held out and had already been seen in round 1.}

\hmodel{Kinetic Ising (model 02). $s_i(t)=\pm1$: agent $i$ talked (talk spin) or acted (activity spin) in 1-min bin $t$; $s_i'=s_i(t{+}1)$. $h_i$: daily-schedule field (removed by a cross-day surrogate). $r_i$: room; $J_{\rm in}$, $J_{\rm out}$: same- and cross-room coupling; a cut predicts $J_{\rm out}\to0$. $\Sigma_g$: Aguilera--Ito--Kolchinsky lower bound on the entropy production $\sigma$, dual weights $\theta$. $\hat R$, $D$: block correlations, variances. $\alpha_{ij}$, $\gamma_d$: pair, day effects.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\footnotesize\setlength{\jot}{1pt}
\begin{gather*}
P(s_i'\,|\,\mathbf s)=\frac{e^{s_i'H_i}}{2\cosh H_i},\quad H_i=h_i+\textstyle\sum_j J_{ij}s_j\\
J_{ij}=J_{\rm in}\,\delta_{r_ir_j}+J_{\rm out}\,(1-\delta_{r_ir_j})\\
\sigma=\textstyle\sum_{i<j}(J_{ij}-J_{ji})\langle g_{ij}\rangle,\quad g_{ij}=s_i's_j-s_is_j'\\
\Sigma_g=\max_\theta\big[\theta\cdot\langle g\rangle-\ln\langle e^{-\theta\cdot g}\rangle\big]\le\sigma\\
J^{\rm MF}=D^{-1/2}\big(I-\hat R^{-1}\big)D^{-1/2}\\
\kappa_{ij,d}=\alpha_{ij}+\gamma_d+\beta\,\mathrm{coloc}_{ij,d}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{Candidly, agents who can't see each other's chat stop timing their chat together: close to a restatement of the scaffold, since rooms are a visibility filter. Three things are mildly useful. (1) A cheap monitor: a pair DiD on 1-min talk spins caught the cut with 18 cut pairs and 5 post-split days (power only about 25--60\%). (2) A negative result an operator splitting teams should know: activity, the actual work, is \emph{not} room-structured. Rooms reorganize conversation, not labor. (3) The unpredicted $\times6$ rise in within-room coupling hints that attention is conserved and redirected to roommates (HH90, now tested by H18). The thermodynamic half, entropy production, is unmeasurable here and buys nothing practical.}

\hbottom{Rooms cut the chat coupling, as the scaffold implies, detectably in a partly blind test; but work is not room-structured, entropy production is unmeasurable at 1-min resolution, and the confirmatory verdict was inconclusive.}
