\hwhat{H09 is an exploratory framing, not one test. It asks whether swarm statistics read as thermodynamics: landscapes, currents, traps and equations of state. Two light non-holdout rounds and round 1b exist; no holdout has run. In round 1 the daily schedule, a field, reproduces the two wells of the activity landscape. Per-agent action chains are irreversible: 78\% of cells beat the null. Regime III has a closed cycle consolidate $\to$ search $\to$ talk $\to$ pause. Event-level entropy production collapses after perma-computer-use. Agents are ergodic at day scale, and restarts relax within a minute. In regime I, room messages raise the escape from idle spells (hazard ratio 3.06; null p95 0.96). In regime III a pause is a self-set timer (0.22\% end early). Round 1b reads the gate on the context ledger: unaddressed chatter keeps agents paused (OR 0.50), and an @-mention releases them (OR 3.1). Memory size is an agent-specific set point that overshoots after forced erasures. Collective entropy production is small (plug-in only).}

\hmodel{No single model: each sub-claim borrows one (models 01, 02, 09, 10). $\lambda(t)$ is the escape hazard of an idle spell at elapsed dwell $t$. HR is the hazard ratio for a kick in the prior 60 s against none. $k_0$ and $c$ are the base escape rate and the kick sensitivity, and $r$ is the kick rate. $t^*$ is the declared pause expiry, and $p_{\rm gate}$ the chance of acting at expiry. $\pi$ and $P$ are the stationary law and transitions of an agent's action-class chain. $V$ is the memory size, $P_{\rm in}$ the inflow, $\beta$ the response and $\gamma$ the relaxation.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\footnotesize\setlength{\jot}{1pt}
\begin{gather*}
\lambda(t)=P(\text{escape at }t\mid\text{idle until }t),\qquad \mathrm{HR}=\lambda_{\rm kick}/\lambda_{\rm none}\\
\text{regime I (Kramers-like):}\quad k\approx k_0+c\,r\\
\text{regime III (timer gate):}\quad \lambda(t)\approx\delta(t-t^*)\,p_{\rm gate}(\text{@-mention})\\
\sigma=\textstyle\sum_{x<y}(\pi_xP_{xy}-\pi_yP_{yx})\ln\dfrac{\pi_xP_{xy}}{\pi_yP_{yx}}\\
\Delta V=\beta P_{\rm in}-\gamma V_{\rm prev}\ \Rightarrow\ V^*=\beta P_{\rm in}/\gamma
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{The thermodynamic vocabulary does little work; the useful findings are plain mechanics. In the current scaffold (regime III) an idle agent sleeps on a timer that it set itself. Unaddressed room chatter keeps it asleep: it acts at 65\% of gates, against 85\% with no news. To wake a timer-paused agent, address it by name, and expect a delay of up to its chosen pause. Regime-I agents were interruptible. Memory size is a set point: consolidation resets it, and storage does not grow. The rest restates the scaffold: schedule-made landscapes, day-scale ergodicity, and a consolidation cycle that is the prompt's own loop. Nothing here yet beats a simpler statistic.}

\hbottom{The thermodynamic framing adds little; the timer gate reads context (chatter holds, an @-mention releases), and memory size is a homeostat that overshoots after forced erasures.}
