\hmodel{Model 09 (Hawkes self-exciting point process). The events are agent messages, with one realization per village day. $\mu_B(t)$ is the schedule baseline: a per-day level times a 30-min shape, plus a kickoff bump. $g_{\text{exo}}$ is the human and nudger input, and $\phi$ is the triggering kernel. The branching ratio $n$ is the mean number of events that one event triggers directly; $n=1$ is critical. $\langle s\rangle$ is the mean cascade size. In the multivariate fit, $\lambda_a$ is agent $a$'s intensity with own and cross kernels. $\bar m$ counts active agents, and $w^{\times}_m$ is the cross weight at timescale $\tau_m$.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
\lambda(t)=\mu_B(t)+\textstyle\sum_{x<t}g_{\text{exo}}(t-x)+\sum_{t_j<t}\phi(t-t_j)\\
\phi(\tau)=n\beta e^{-\beta\tau},\qquad \langle s\rangle=1/(1-n)\\
\lambda_a(t)=c_a\mu(t)+\textstyle\sum_{t_j\in a}\phi_{\text{self}}(t-t_j)+\sum_{t_j\notin a}\phi_{\times}(t-t_j)\\
n_\times=(\bar m-1)\textstyle\sum_{\tau_m\le300\,\text{s}}w^{\times}_m
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{The swarm is far from a runaway cascade, and more agents or a shared goal do not push it closer. A monitor for message storms can assume $\hat n\le0.4$ in this scaffold: one message triggers fewer than 0.4 direct follow-ups. The scheduler and the roster set the activity level, not social contagion. The one stable coupling is about 0.07 extra messages from others within 30 s of each message. That number can be a scaffold property: agents wake when a message lands. Do not fit a constant-baseline Hawkes model to agent logs. It gives $\hat n\approx0.99$ where a schedule baseline gives 0.2--0.4.}

\hbottom{The swarm is subcritical ($n\approx0.2$--$0.4$), its self-excitation does not depend on the goal's coupling mode, and only a small, fast cross-triggering survives its null.}
