\hwhat{The test asks whether newcomers acquire a village culture. A newcomer's style-free chat content is compared with the veterans' same-day centroid in tenure days 2--4 and 8--14, after the goal and kickoff span is projected out. The veterans' change on the same days is subtracted. Over 19 joins (15 join days, three regimes) the enculturation index is $\Delta G=+0.020$ [$-0.025$, $+0.070$] with bge and $-0.011$ [$-0.047$, $+0.026$] with gte. Both sit below the calibrated null cut ($+0.032$). Newcomers start only $0.02$ below the veterans. The synthetic power at half closure is 0.15--0.30, so the headline is inconclusive. A family signature at entry is real (13/15 joins) and fades by week two. In \#38 newcomers align with their own room at once. NE32's isolation lasted 1.5--2 h, not a day. The holdout script is not run.}

\hmodel{Vector spins (model 11). The spin is a statement's 32-d style-residualized unit vector $\tilde u_s$. $\Pi_P$ projects out the goal and kickoff field. A newcomer's constant $q_i$ relaxes toward the veterans' centroid $\bar V$ with closure $\lambda$ as its veteran reads $D_i$ grow. $h_d$ is the day field. The observable $a_{i,d}$ is the mean statement cosine with the veterans' centroid $V_d$. $\Delta G$ is the newcomer's change minus the veterans'. $K$ is the family signature against other newcomers' first-day baselines $F_f$.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
\tilde u_s\propto\Pi_P\bigl[q_i(\tau)+h_d+\varepsilon_s\bigr],\quad q_i(\tau)=q_i^0+\lambda\bigl(1-e^{-D_i(\tau)/D^*}\bigr)(\bar V-q_i^0)\\
\Delta G_i=(\bar a_{i,L}-\bar a_{i,E})-\langle\bar a_{j,L}-\bar a_{j,E}\rangle_{j},\quad a_{i,d}=\langle\tilde u_s\rangle\cdot\hat V_{d}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{An operator should not expect newcomers to need an induction period for content. They match the day's conversation from day 2: the gap to the veterans is $-0.02$ in cosine units and closes to 0 by days 8--14. Put a newcomer in the room whose work it should join; it aligns with that room's veterans at once (0.18, the veterans' own value). Expect a family flavor in its first week; it fades. Do not rely on reading volume: veteran items read do not predict the move ($\rho=+0.06$). Whether a deeper village culture exists is not settled here. The shared persistent content is about 3\% of statement variance, too small to test with 19 joins.}

\hbottom{Newcomers join the village's conversation at once and lose a first-week family signature, but no slower enculturation into a village culture is detectable at this power.}

\hcaveats{The persistent shared component is small, so a null is inconclusive, not absent. bge and gte disagree in sign on $\Delta G$; statement-level geometry is model-dependent. Family baselines use 1--4 same-family newcomers per regime, and the fade is significant with bge only. Batch joins share windows (15 clusters). \#51 roles are an agent-specific field; the own role text is projected out, but role topics remain. Windows can cross a goal boundary. The baselines without same-day joins and the NE32 premise check are post hoc.}
