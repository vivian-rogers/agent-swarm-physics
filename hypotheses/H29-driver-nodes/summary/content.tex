Driver score $D_k$: reachability of the swarm mean from agent $k$ over $T=4$\,h. $E_k=N^2/D_k$: energy to shift the mean through $k$.}

\htelos{To move one agent, name it: named messages pull 5--25$\times$ more than unnamed ones in \#51, and broadcast content barely propagates. An operator message to one agent stays with that agent (NE38). If you must pick a relay, rank agents by net reply current (replies received minus given), not by volume or content-pull ``driver'' scores. Expect a weak edge (held-out $\rho\approx0.1$--0.2). A designated leader gets $\times1.39$ replies per message, not more reach (\#35). Network controllability adds nothing measurable; the steering-cost scaling ($\propto N^{2.7}$ for a typical agent) is model-only.}

\hbottom{Content influence is real and address-gated (named $\gg$ unnamed on the context ledger); the content-pull driver ranking fails, but a reply-graph ranking weakly predicts spread.}

\hcaveats{The positive findings are post hoc (boundary test, Amendment 2) or new in round 1b (the reply network, chosen by the re-evaluation brief).
