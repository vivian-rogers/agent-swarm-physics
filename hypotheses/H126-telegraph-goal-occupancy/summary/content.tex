\hwhat{HH367 models each agent's on-goal state as a random telegraph signal. In that model dwells are exponential, two mean dwells predict the occupancy $p=k_{\rm on}/(k_{\rm on}+k_{\rm off})$, and a kickoff raises only $k_{\rm on}$. H126 fits a two-state hidden Markov model to H105's decoy-threshold on-goal labels on each agent's ledger call clock: 39 non-holdout units, 17 kickoffs, the \#12 debate schedule, \#51 private goals and Opus~5's reassignment. The decisive result is that a kickoff lowers $k_{\rm off}$ as well as raising $k_{\rm on}$: $\Delta\ln k_{\rm off}$ is below 0 in 15 of 17 kickoffs. The dwell-shape and occupancy clauses are not identifiable at village sampling (synthetic, before data).}

\hmodel{Telegraph spin read through a noisy channel (models 10 and 02). The latent $s_{i,c}\in\{{\rm off},{\rm on}\}$ of agent $i$ switches per ledger call with $a_i=k_{\rm on}$ and $b_i=k_{\rm off}$. Statements are observed at irregular call gaps $\Delta$ and labelled on-goal with sensitivity $q_1$ and false-positive rate $q_0$. Rival: a 4-state aggregated chain whose dwells are mixtures of two geometrics. Kickoff designs fit segment rates before and after the boundary with shared emissions.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}

P(b_k{=}1\mid s)=q_0+(q_1-q_0)\,\mathbb 1[s={\rm on}],\qquad \tau_{\rm on}=1/b,\ \tau_{\rm off}=1/a\\

\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator, a goal works by holding agents on goal as much as by recruiting them. A kickoff multiplies the per-call chance of switching onto the goal by about 5 and divides the chance of leaving it by about 6 (medians over 17 kickoffs). Latent on-goal stints last a median of 78 calls. Expect occupancy to respond to anything that interrupts agents (lower $k_{\rm off}$ is what a goal buys), not only to how often it is announced.}

\hbottom{An assigned goal acts on both telegraph rates: it raises the on-rate and lowers the off-rate ($\Delta\ln k_{\rm off}<0$ in 15/17 kickoffs and in the \#12 debate schedule), so it is a symmetric field, not an attempt-rate field. Dwell shape is not identifiable here.}

\hcaveats{The on-goal label is a text-embedding proxy (bge decoy threshold); gte doubles the heavy-tail counts. In each kickoff's pre-segment, occupancy along the next goal sits at the decoy floor ($p\approx0.04$), so its $k_{\rm off}$ rests on few latent stints, although the synthetic gave 0/30 false $k_{\rm off}$ calls under a true $k_{\rm on}$-only change. Sparse regime-III units give unstable short dwells. A kickoff smoke test printed one result (K38) after Amendment A1 and before the full run.}
