\hwhat{HH337 asks whether H100's spontaneous \#best/\#rest content split is a true symmetry breaking or a hidden field. True symmetry breaking starts at zero on day 1 and grows, with a direction chosen by early fluctuations. A hidden field, such as the work each room inherits, gives a full split on day 1 along a direction the members' previous repos predict. H107 measures the day-1 split against the final one in 8 non-holdout two-room periods, with the inherited repo field as an explicit covariate. \textbf{Neither picture covers all four identical-kickoff weeks.} In \#37 and \#39 the split starts near zero and grows. In \#41 and \#42 it is at 70--90\% of final size on day 1, but the day-1 direction is only half kept. Inherited repos set the day-1 direction in \#42 only.}

\hmodel{Model 11 (soft-spin O($n$), $n=32$ whitened dimensions), with the inherited work as a model-10 Potts field. $\mathbf{x}_{i,b}$ is agent $i$'s style-residualized content in time bin $b$, centred on the bin and minus its leave-period-out constant. $\boldsymbol{\Delta}$ is the room order parameter. $E(b)$ is its squared size in excess of random joint relabels. The inherited field $\mathbf{h}_r$ weights each repo's content vector $\mathbf{v}_\sigma$ by the members' pre-period commit shares $w_{i\sigma}$.}

\htelos{For an operator, this mostly says what not to infer. The room difference you see on day 1 of a week with identical instructions is a poor guide to the week: in some weeks it is absent and grows, and where it exists its direction is only about half kept ($c_1\approx0.5$). The work the members bring in rarely sets it, which weakens the ``hidden field'' explanation of H100's residual without proving true symmetry breaking. Day resolution cannot separate a field present at $t=0$ from an instability that grows within two hours. This is a measurement of onset shape, not a lever.}

\hbottom{The room content split has no single onset shape: it grows from zero in two identical-kickoff weeks and is nearly full on day 1 in two others, its day-1 direction is half kept, and inherited repos set it only on one week's first day.}
