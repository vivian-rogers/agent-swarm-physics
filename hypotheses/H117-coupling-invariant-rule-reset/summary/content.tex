\hwhat{HH355 says that a pure field change (a rule reset, a tool change, a new goal) moves each agent's field $h_i$ but not the couplings $J_{ij}$. A fitted $J$ that jumps at such a step is a field artifact. The target, NE35 in \#32, is held out. H117 fits a kinetic Ising model to minute talk spins on both sides of 9 non-holdout steps (2 days each side). It compares the change in $J$ with 109 placebo splits at ordinary day boundaries. \textbf{$J$ stays at its placebo level, but most steps are not field steps in the data.} The coupling shift $Q$ exceeds the placebo 95th percentile at 1 of 9 steps (NE17, 7 pairs, $p=0.037$, no field shift). The field shift $F$ beats day-to-day changes at one step only: the \#41$\to$\#42 goal switch. There $Q=0.51$ ($p=0.74$). The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Model 02, kinetic Ising with parallel updates. A talk spin $s_i(t)=\pm1$ marks whether agent $i$ posted chat in minute $t$. Each agent's next spin is a logistic function of its own spin, the 5-min boxcar $m_j$ of every other agent, and a per-agent field for each (day, 30-min block). The fields absorb the schedule and any field step. Under HH355, $h_i$ moves across a step while $J$ does not. A second estimator works on the call clock. It compares messages read just before a call ($J^R$) with messages posted during it and not yet read ($J^U$).}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
\mathrm{logit}\,P(s_i^{t+1}=1)=h_i+\delta_i(b)+K_is_i^t+K'_im_i^t+\textstyle\sum_{j}J_{ij}m_j^t\\
Q=\big\langle (J^s_{\mathrm{after}}-J^s_{\mathrm{before}})^2/(\sigma^2_a+\sigma^2_b)\big\rangle_{\text{fixed pairs}},\quad J^s=\tfrac12(J_{ij}+J_{ji})\\
F=\big\langle (\bar h_a-\bar h_b)^2/(\sigma^2_a+\sigma^2_b)\big\rangle_i
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator: a goal or rule change does not rewire who responds to whom in talk timing. At \#38-type sizes, a change of $\pm1.2$ logistic units on half the pairs would be seen with probability 0.85, and none is seen. Talk couplings carry over a reset. Only room moves changed them, and those changes were small (switched pairs $Q=0.93$ against fixed pairs 0.61). For an analyst: a fitted $J$ on talk spins with block fields is not a field artifact at this resolution. Most documented rule changes, however, do not move talk fields more than an ordinary night does.}
