\hwhat{I split the irreversibility of the swarm's behavior mix into excess (net change of the mix) and housekeeping (cycles with no net change). The split follows Kolchinsky et al.\ (2026) and uses ensemble fluxes of Jev v3 states over the agents present at each time, in G51, G38 and G40. The synthetic check came first. In every period, 80--100\% of the excess falls in the day's first and last 30\,min, against a 12--25\% time share. The final block alone holds 72--100\%. DQ8 trimming removes 81--100\% of it. Kickoffs add no excess beyond an ordinary day start. The synthetic predicted this: a 5-h relaxation is too slow to show. Housekeeping does not separate from a reversible surrogate. A forced erasure gives a one-window excess pulse ($\times5$--12). Holdout: not run.}

\hmodel{Models 02 (kinetic jump process) and 05 (driven population). The agents present in a 5-min window are exchangeable copies of a Markov chain on five behavior states: absent, work, explore, coord, wait. The fine version uses the 11 v3 states plus absent. $j_{xy}$ is the soft transition flux per agent-step in a 30-min block. $\sigma$ is the total EP, $\sigma_{\rm ex}$ the excess and $\sigma_{\rm hk}$ the housekeeping, all in nats per agent-step. The scheduler's start and stop and the kickoff are fields.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
\sigma=\textstyle\sum_{x\ne y} j_{xy}\ln\frac{j_{xy}}{j_{yx}},\qquad \dot p_x=\sum_y (j_{yx}-j_{xy})\\
\sigma_{\rm ex}=\max_{\phi}\Big[-\phi^{\!\top}\dot p-\textstyle\sum_{x\ne y} j_{xy}\big(e^{\phi_y-\phi_x}-1\big)\Big]\ \approx\ \dot p^{\top}H^{+}\dot p\\
\sigma_{\rm hk}=\sigma-\sigma_{\rm ex}\ \ge0,\qquad \sigma_{\rm ex}=0\iff\dot p=0
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{Mostly a measurement lesson, with one operator number. The behavior mix of an LLM swarm changes irreversibly at the scheduler's stop. Agents finish at staggered times, and that carries 72--100\% of a day's excess. Any irreversibility or EP statistic on agent behavior must therefore trim to the all-present window first, or it measures the cron table (H38). A kickoff quench is invisible in 5-min behavior states: use content or repo allocation to see it (H54, H75). Housekeeping, the work cycle that could gauge real activity, is below the noise floor with soft labels at $N\le27$. For a monitor this gives a scheduler gauge, not a work gauge.}

\hbottom{The swarm's excess behavioral irreversibility is the scheduler's day end, which trimming removes; kickoffs and the housekeeping work cycle are below the detection floor of a 12--27-agent ensemble with soft labels.}
