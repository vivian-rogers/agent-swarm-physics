\hwhat{H94 asks whether the agent $\times$ repo allocation of DQ4 work is a maximum-entropy (Foley) equilibrium given its constraints. The data are 24 testable units in \#31--\#51, in 30-min work quanta grouped into episodes. A hierarchy of max-ent models adds agent activity, repo size, ownership and rooms. After these constraints, the information left above the run-persistence floor is $\le0.13$ of $I(\text{agent};\text{repo})$ in 23 of 24 units, and inside the floor's 95\% band in 12. Ownership is a price set by the goal: median 2.2 nats in shared weeks, 7.8 in own-role units ($p=0.0007$). Agents do not specialize beyond ownership. Episode concentration is at or above neutral copying in shared weeks; kickoff-named repos explain it in 2 of 6. The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Models 10 and 05: a max-ent model of a categorical allocation, with repos as replicator abundances. A work quantum is one agent $\times$ 30-min window $\times$ repo with an agent commit; an episode is a run of quanta on one repo. The owner of a repo is its first committer in DQ4. $\lambda_{\rm own}$ and $\lambda_{\rm room}$ are Lagrange multipliers (prices, in nats). $D_k$ is the KL divergence from the level-$k$ max-ent table. $\kappa$ places the repo-size law between Maxwell--Boltzmann (0, even) and Bose--Einstein (1, neutral P\'olya copying).}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
P_k(i,j)\propto a_i\,b_j\,e^{\lambda_{\rm own}\,\mathrm{own}_{ij}+\lambda_{\rm room}\,\mathrm{room}_{ij}}\\
D_k=\mathrm{KL}(\hat P\,\|\,P_k),\quad D_1=I(\text{agent};\text{repo}),\quad D_{k-1}-D_k=\mathrm{KL}(P_k\|P_{k-1})\\
\kappa=\frac{H_{\rm MB}-H_{\rm obs}}{H_{\rm MB}-H_{\rm BE}}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{Work allocation in these swarms is statistically featureless once you know who works how much, which repos are big, who owns what and which room each agent sits in. There is no hidden plan to recover and no extra specialization. A monitor can therefore summarize allocation with two numbers per week: the ownership price $\lambda_{\rm own}$ and the concentration $\kappa$. The price is a lever: own-artifact goals raise it from about 2 to 8 nats, and a merge into one shared target (NE42) lowers it by $\ge7$ nats. To spread work, assign owners; to pool work, name one shared repo in the kickoff. Herding beyond neutral copying appears in 5 of 7 shared weeks, but kickoff naming accounts for it only twice.}

\hbottom{Work episodes are allocated at maximum entropy given agent activity, repo sizes, ownership and rooms; the ownership price is a goal-set field (2.2 nats in shared weeks, 7.8 in own-role units).}
