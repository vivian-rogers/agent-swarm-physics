\hwhat{An \emph{ideology egregore} is a memeplex: a set of content elements that many agents express together, whose hosts change while the pattern persists, and whose own state predicts its next state beyond the fields that act on the agents. H145 discovers memeplexes in \#51 (45 non-reserved days, 32 agents, 8 labs) from 2,011 elements: meaning clusters, coined names and rare words, cleaned shared repos and touched projects. It then tests host renewal and Krakauer colonial individuality at the pattern level. \textbf{Result:} 15 cross-lab memeplexes exist beyond chance timing. Two or three renew their hosts. One broad topic community (K03) carries 0.20 bits per 2-h transition beyond the coded fields. The field control is uninformative, so K03 may still be a leaked field.}

\hmodel{Models 12 (information dynamics) and 13 (conventions). Element expression $x_{i,e}(b)\in\{0,1\}$; hosts $H_K(b)=\{i:\sum_{e\in K}x_{i,e}(b)\ge2\}$; state $S_K(b)$ = host level $\times$ which third of $K$ dominates (13 symbols). Discovery: positive PMI of co-expression beyond each agent's vocabulary and each agent-bin's activity (A1), Benjamini--Hochberg edges, Louvain $\gamma=1$. $E$: scheduler phase, exogenous messages and their similarity to $K$, role-text field, rest-of-village activity, lagged one bin.}

\htelos{For an operator: the village's shared vocabulary organizes into about 15 large cross-lab topic communities, each carried by 15--31 agents on almost every day. Most are carried by the same agents day after day. Their next state is predictable from the schedule, operator input and role texts, not from the pattern itself. One broad engagement-and-papers community (K03) adds 0.20 bits per 2-h step beyond those inputs, in the second half of the period only. Do not treat the named norms (verification, consent, onboarding) as self-sustaining ideologies on this evidence.}

\hbottom{Memeplexes exist in \#51 beyond chance; at most one is a pattern-level individual, and it may be a leaked field.}

\hcaveats{Exploration data only; the reserved tail 51m is untouched. The first discovery (old rules, old cleaner) was seen before the amendments. Labels come from a token rule, and the large communities mix several story families. $E$ is coarse (tertiles), and exogenous input falls in 94\% of bins. K03's pass is post hoc confined to days 23--45 and to $m\ge2$, and a rerun of the null gives $z$ 2.67. The 30-min and 1-day tests are not synthetic-validated. Reserved \#45--\#50 were read for vocabulary by a helper and are not used.}
