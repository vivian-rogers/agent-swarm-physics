\hwhat{HH354 asked whether winning the \#26 election steps up the winner's out-coupling: after the result at 19:35 UTC on 2026-01-05, do the other nine agents talk more often after reading agent 17? In 83-min windows on both sides the step is $+0.09\pm0.33$ Ising units, at the 56th percentile of 16 time placebos and the 71st of a field-only skeleton null. \hmodel{Model 02 on the call clock. Spin $s_j(c)=\pm1$: call $c$ of agent $j$ talks or not. Input $x_{jw}(c)=1$ if the winner $w$ posted in $j$'s room since $j$'s previous call (read-gated); in-flight analogs serve as placebos. Fields: agent $\times$ call mode $\times$ side (pre/post), which absorb the new goal as a field. Couplings: $J_{out}$ (readers' response to $w$), $J_{in}$ ($w$'s response to others), each with a post-result step $\Delta$.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
P(s_j(c)=+1)=\frac{e^{H_j}}{2\cosh H_j},\quad H_j=h_{j,\kappa,\mathrm{side}}+J_{jj}s_j(c^-)+(J_{out}+\Delta J_{out}\,\mathrm{post})\,x_{jw}+(J_{oth}+\Delta J_{oth}\,\mathrm{post})\,X_{j,-w}\\
\text{detect}\iff\Delta J_{out}\ge0.05\ \wedge\ \Delta J_{out}>q_{95}(\text{time placebos})\ \wedge\ \Delta J_{out}>q_{95}(\text{skeleton null})
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator: electing or appointing a leader does not, by itself, make the other agents answer that agent more per call. A step of $\geq0.75$ Ising units is excluded at the election, and judges and lead designers show none ($-0.04\pm0.10$, $-0.02\pm0.11$). The \#26 leader did become the top talk source on later days and after the re-election, which is consistent with reach earned by messages rather than granted by the title. In this village an appointment acts as a field (it changes what agents work on). One event cannot resolve the 0.05 step HH354 asked about.}
