\hwhat{H93 fits project choice as a Brock--Durlauf (BD) logit with a share coupling $\beta J$, per period, on DQ4 work arrivals and attention labels in 13 periods (\#30--\#51). Multiple equilibria were predicted to split same-type weeks. The estimator M4 controls kickoff naming, habit and repo size; it passes the synthetic false-positive rule on all 13 schedules. $\hat{\beta J}>0$ (CI $>0$) in 5 of 10 identified work periods, $+1.9$ to $+5.8$. It is negative in the own-role weeks (\#51 $-11.7$ [$-19.0$, $-4.4$]). A post hoc synthetic shows that fast repo bursts without coupling give the same values. Every period lies below its multiplicity boundary ($\hat{\beta J}/\gamma_c\le0.86$). No same-field replica lands in a second state. The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Model 10 (mean-field Potts) in its econ form, with model 05's repo fitness as the confound. An agent's state is its current repo or attention project. A choice event is a switch or a resumption. $s_j$ is the share of the other active agents on option $j$. The fields are kickoff naming $\mathrm{nm}_j$ (H54), habit $\mathrm{prev}_{ij}$ (the agent held $j$ before) and log cumulative arrivals $c_j$ (fitness proxy). $\beta J$ is the coupling in noise units. $\gamma_c$ is the coupling at which the fitted fields first support two stable states.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
P(j\mid e)\propto\exp\!\big(b_n\,\mathrm{nm}_j+b_o\,\mathrm{prev}_{ij}+b_c\ln(1{+}c_j)+\beta J\,s_j\big)\\
m_j=\big\langle\mathrm{softmax}_j(h_{i\cdot}+\beta J\,m)\big\rangle_i,\quad\text{stable: }\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda(\partial F/\partial m)<1\\
\gamma_c=\min\{\beta J:\ \#\text{stable fixed points}\ge2\}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{Agents do pile onto projects that others already work on: from 0 to a third of the agents multiplies the pick odds by about 2--7 in shared weeks. That term cannot be told apart from a common burst of repo attention, so do not read it as imitation. The useful result is negative: at the fitted fields no week is bistable. An operator need not fear that a free week will lock into a herd by chance. Herding follows the fields: what the kickoff names, which team is assigned, and habit, which is the largest term (odds $\times$12--400 of returning to one's own repo). To spread work, change the fields (assign owners, as in \#51), not the chat.}

\hbottom{Project choice carries a share coefficient of $+2$ to $+6$ in shared weeks and $-6$ to $-12$ in own-role weeks, but repo bursts mimic it; at the fitted fields no period is bistable and no same-field replica splits.}
