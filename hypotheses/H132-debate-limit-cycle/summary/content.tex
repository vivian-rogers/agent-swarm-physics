\hwhat{HH375 proposed that a debate is a two-sublattice limit cycle: each team rebuts the other's last move, so the issue content alternates sign at lag one and the cycle runs one way. H132 tests this in \#12's ten Asian-Parliamentary debates (306 turns, drafted Government and Opposition teams). Each turn is projected on the motion's support--oppose axis, built from the motion text without labels, and each team's static position is removed. The content does not alternate. Consecutive cross-team turns are \emph{positively} correlated ($\rho_1=+0.16$, shuffle $p=0.002$), and the alternation $\Lambda=\rho_2-\rho_1=+0.05$ sits inside the turn-shuffled null ($p=0.08$). The kill fires; the synthetic power for $k_\times=0.6$ is 1.00. The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Alternating-update two-sublattice linear dynamics (model 02) with antiferromagnetic block coupling (model 01). $y_t$ is turn $t$'s whitened projection on the issue axis $\hat a_d$; $\epsilon_t=\pm1$ marks Government or Opposition; $\mu_d$ is the assigned (staggered) field; $u_t$ is the dynamic issue state with antiferromagnetic response $k_\times$; $z_t$ removes each team's mean. Speakers alternate by format, so only $z_t$ can carry a cycle.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
y_t=\epsilon_t\mu_d+a_{i(t)}+u_t+e_t,\qquad u_{t+1}=-k_\times u_t+\eta_{t+1}\\
\rho_1\approx-k_\times r,\quad \rho_2\approx k_\times^2 r,\quad \Lambda=\rho_2-\rho_1\\
A_\gamma=\rho_{G\to O}(1)-\rho_{O\to G}(1),\qquad L=\langle a_t g_{t+1}-g_t a_{t+1}\rangle
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator running adversarial or debate formats: assigned sides show up as a static tilt in content, and turn-to-turn content persists across the team line. Expect a rebuttal to restate the point it answers (lag-one correlation $\approx+0.16$ on the issue axis, $+0.15$ in full content), not to swing the topic back and forth. Use stance labels, not embeddings, to see opposition between moves.}

\hcaveats{Embeddings encode negation poorly, so a rebuttal that restates a claim reads as agreement on the issue axis; the antiferromagnet H21 and H37 found lives in reply stance. Cycles weaker than $k_\times\approx0.4$ at H21-sized noise are not excluded (power 0.74 at 0.3). A staggered-field ramp gives false $\Lambda$ in 10\% of synthetic worlds. The negative $L$ in all variants is post hoc. Ten debates, one period, regime I.}
