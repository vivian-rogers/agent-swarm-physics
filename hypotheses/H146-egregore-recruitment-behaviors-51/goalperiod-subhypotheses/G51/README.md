## Why this period

H145 discovers the memeplexes here; H146 tests whether they recruit, survive amnesia and carry functional behaviors.

## Prediction
*Written 2026-10-09, before running on this period (card §Prediction, Amendment A1).*
- P1/P1b: read − in-flight log-OR > 0 (CI above 0) for ≥ 2 memeplexes, beating their pseudo-patterns (p_pseudo ≤ 0.05). Declared before any estimate: K1 cannot fire here (per-pattern power ≤ 0.57 at log-OR 0.3), so a null is inconclusive.
- P2: for patterns that pass P1/P1b, named-read excess ≥ 3× unnamed.
- P3: newcomers' first-2-day expression follows reads. Declared untestable (power ≤ 0.12); descriptive only.
- P4: host-wipe re-expression HR ≥ 0.8 of placebo; read-gated re-expression faster. K2 fires if HR < 0.5.
- P5: named K messages to a host rise after its wipe, challenge or lapse (RR > 1.2, CI above 1) for ≥ 1 pattern; K3 also needs p_pseudo ≤ 0.05.
- P6: specialization z ≥ 2 for ≥ 2 patterns; amended Ω rule (A1.9) for ≥ 1.
- Counts against: P1/P1b positives explained by the hub (no-hub refit) or by Claude Fable 5's relayed input; P4 HR < 0.5; P5 and P6 no better than pseudo-patterns.
