\hwhat{HH340 says that an agent's own repo acts like the pinned layer in exchange bias. Agents still committing to their own repo at a goal boundary should keep the old goal state longer (decay $\ge2\times$ slower), and the offset should end within a day of their last commit to that repo. H110 re-implements H96's field-orthogonal old-state remanence per agent and splits it by DQ4 pinning at 7 non-holdout transitions (4 with both groups). \textbf{A small day-1 asymmetry, no exchange bias.} Pinned agents keep $R_1=0.17$ vs $-0.13$ (difference 0.30 [$-0.01$, 0.57]; gte 0.22), but the offset is not tied to continued commits, is gone by day 2, and reverses under an any-repo definition. Neither HH340's rule nor its kill fires. The holdout (incl.\ NE24) is not run.}

\hmodel{Model 01 (hysteresis under a stepped field) measured with the O(32) content vector of model 11. A free spin's old-state component decays as $m_i(h)=m_i(0)e^{-\lambda h}$ (H96: a quench, $R_1\approx0.25$). A spin bonded to a pinned layer feels an extra field $J_\mathrm{ex}\pi_i(h)$ while it still commits to its own repo ($\pi=1$), which shifts its loop: slower decay, and a residual offset that ends with the last commit.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
\frac{dm_i}{dh}=-\lambda\big(m_i-m_i^\infty\big),\qquad m_i^\infty=J_\mathrm{ex}\,\pi_i(h)/\lambda\\
R_1^g=\frac{\sum_{i\in g}m_i(\mathrm{day}\,1)}{\sum_{i\in g}m_i(\mathrm{pre})},\qquad \rho_\lambda=\frac{\ln\max(R_1^U,0.02)}{\ln\max(R_1^P,0.02)}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator: do not expect agents with a live own repo to resist a goal change. By day 2 they have switched like everyone else (difference 0.11 of the pre level, CI includes 0). The kickoff alone moves them; archiving their old repo is not needed for the content switch. The own repo stores where an agent works (H70), not what it is thinking about.}

\hbottom{An agent's own live repo does not pin its old goal state: at most a small day-1 residual (0.30 [$-0.01$, 0.57] of the pre level) that is not tied to continued commits and is gone by day 2.}
