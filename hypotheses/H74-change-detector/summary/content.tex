\hwhat{H74 builds a daily change detector for an agent swarm and scores it against 217 dated changes on 282 non-reserved days (April 2025 to September 2026). Round 1: a five-channel fused alarm was too noisy (18\% false alarms on quiet days); a schema diff of the log records was precise. \textbf{Round 2} keeps three channels: the schema diff S, operator counters D3 (bookends, nudges, human messages) and H36's frozen topic rule C3. Thresholds are calibrated on quiet days of the \emph{other} goal periods, so every false-alarm rate (FAR) is out-of-sample. The monitor has FAR 0.085 [0.04, 0.17] per quiet day and hits goal kickoffs at 0.57 ($p$ 0.003) and room changes at 6/6. D3 dates all three undocumented drive steps (NE39, NE43a, NE43b) at FAR 1/82. Scaffold tool changes are hit at chance. Verdict: \textbf{mixed}.}

\hmodel{Models 02 and 11. A day $d$ is one step of a driven system. A platform step changes record schemas for every agent at once; an operator step changes drive counters; a goal step is a field step on content. Counts are heavy-tailed, so each counter is logged, scored by a robust trailing $z$ (10 days) and must persist for two days. Each channel $k$ alarms above $\theta_k(g)$, the conformal $(1-\alpha)$ quantile of its scores on the $n$ placebo days outside goal period $g$ (placebo: $\ge2$ active days from every catalogued event).}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
p_f(d)=\min\{|z_f(d-1)|,|z_f(d)|\},\quad z_f=z[\log(1+x_f)],\quad D3(d)=\max_f p_f(d)\\
\theta_k(g)=s_{k,(\lceil(1-\alpha)(n+1)\rceil)}\ \text{over placebo days}\notin g,\qquad \alpha=0.02\\
\text{alarm}(d)=[z_S>\theta_S]\lor[D3>\theta_{D}]\lor[\mathrm{R1}\ge3\lor Z_{\rm cont}\ge2]
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}
