\hwhat{H22 finds that rival agents in \#51 co-move in \emph{topic}, so topic cannot show conflict. H37 tests whether \emph{stance} shows it. A zero-shot labeller (Jev) marks 30k reply pairs in \#12, \#26, \#40 and \#51. In the \#12 debates, stance separates opponents from teammates (AUC 0.74 after agent effects), and topic does not (0.48). Stance recovers the drafted teams exactly in 7 of 10 debates, and the contrast vanishes after the verdict. \#51 rivals and opposed roles are as friendly as anyone, and \#26 stance ignores ballots. \textbf{Round 1c} re-tests this on ledger-visible DQ2 replies with DQ10's validated disagreement flag (precision 0.61--0.67, recall $\approx0.6$). with its noise in every null. \#12 opponents disagree in 33 of 74 replies and teammates in 0 of 60 (stress null $p=0.001$). \#40 now raises no false alarm. \#51 rivals and opposed roles show no excess disagreement. The \#34 saboteur test is \textbf{not run}; a re-freeze on held-out v2.1 labels is proposed.}

\hmodel{Models 01 (signed Ising) and 10 (Potts communities). The spin is a bond: the stance $s_e\in[-1,1]$ of reply $e$ from $j$ to $i$, from Jev probabilities. $a_j$ and $b_i$ are agent fields (agreeableness, likability) of an ordered logit with cut points $c_k$. $\Delta$ is the camp coupling. $J_{ij}$ is the residual pair stance after the fields, and $\xi_i=\pm1$ is the camp. Factions are the ground state of $H(\xi)$; frustration $f$ is the weight it leaves unsatisfied. $\gamma$ is same-team ($\delta^{\rm team}_{ij}=1$) minus opposite-team stance, with fields absorbed.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
P(\sigma_e\le k)=\operatorname{logistic}\!\big(c_k-a_j-b_i-\Delta\,\xi_i\xi_j\big),\quad \sigma_e\in\{-,0,+\}\\
s_e=p_{\rm agree}+p_{\rm support}-p_{\rm oppose}-p_{\rm undermine}\\
s_e=\mu+a_j+b_i+\gamma\,\delta^{\rm team}_{ij}+\varepsilon_e\\
H(\boldsymbol\xi)=-\textstyle\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}\,\xi_i\xi_j\\
f=\tfrac12\Big(1-\max_{\boldsymbol\xi}\big[-H(\boldsymbol\xi)\big]\big/\textstyle\sum_{i<j}|J_{ij}|\Big)
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{To find assigned sides or a scapegoated agent, run a stance labeller on replies. It costs about \$1.12 per 30k reply pairs and recovers debate teams exactly in 7/10 debates. Check every camp alarm against the agent-field (ordered-logit) null, which holds 4\% false alarms; the sign-shuffle null fires falsely in 28--100\%. LLM agents almost never take sides unless a protocol tells them to, so the detector mostly confirms that nothing happens. Whether it catches covert sabotage is the open \#34 question. Do not monitor the raw ``negative reply'' rate: it is mostly correction friction. Use the validated disagreement flag, corrected for precision $\approx0.64$ and recall $\approx0.6$.}

\hbottom{A validated disagreement flag sees the conflict a debate assigns (45\% vs 0\% of replies; topic blind), and rival and opposed agents show none: disagreement lives only where imposed.}
