\hwhat{I measured whether the swarm's joint dynamics are more irreversible than its agents' own dynamics. The estimator is the Aguilera--Ito--Kolchinsky max-ent bound on DQ8-trimmed windows, in three channels (5-min behavior states, 1-min talk, 1-min activity), on eight regime-III periods. Synthetic validation came first. In G51 (45 days, 21--32 agents) the talk channel carries a collective arrow of time: $\sigma_{\rm nam}=7.6\,[4.3,11.2]\times10^{-3}$ nats/min on named partners, none on unnamed partners (block-shift $p=0.01$). Single-agent talk is reversible, so this arrow is fully collective. Behavior is irreversible per agent ($0.078$ nats per 5 min) with a collective share of $0.003$. The seven smaller periods are underpowered. Pairwise directions do not follow who names whom. Holdout (\#51 tail): not run.}

\hmodel{Models 15 (EP inference) and 02 (kinetic spins with asymmetric couplings). Spins: an agent's behavior state (5 soft states) or its talk or activity in a minute. Single observables $u_i$: one agent's time-antisymmetric patterns. Coupling observables $g_i$: agent $i$'s state against a partner field $h_i$, the mean over agents who named $i$ (named), never named either way (unnamed), or all others. $\Sigma_g$: the max-ent lower bound on entropy production for observables $g$; $\theta$: its multipliers.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
\Sigma\ \ge\ \Sigma_g=\max_\theta\big[\theta^\top\langle g\rangle-\ln\langle e^{-\theta^\top g}\rangle\big]\\
\Sigma_g\approx 2\langle g\rangle^{\!\top}K^{-1}\langle g\rangle\quad(\text{held out by day fold})\\
g_i(t)=s_i(t{+}1)\,h_i(t)-s_i(t)\,h_i(t{+}1)\\
h_i^{\rm nam}=\textstyle\sum_j w_{ji}s_j\big/\sum_j w_{ji}\quad(w_{ji}:\ j\text{ names }i)\\
\sigma_{\rm coll}=\Sigma_{u\cup g}-\Sigma_u\ge 0,\qquad \Delta_{\rm addr}=\sigma_{\rm nam}-\sigma_{\rm un}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{Two uses, both modest. First, a monitor that wants to detect agents coordinating should look at talk timing between agents that name each other: that is where the swarm's only joint arrow of time sits, at about 0.017 nats per agent-hour in \#51. Second, a method warning: a block-shift or cross-day null does not separate coupling from a common drive with agent-specific lags; split partners by addressing instead. The detector needs about 40 days and 25 agents. On a 5-day, 15-agent week it has no power (power $\le0.05$ at the \#51 effect size). There is no egregore here: removing the naming partition removes the collective term.}

\hbottom{The swarm's only measurable collective arrow of time is agents talking in the minute after the agents who named them talked; it is coupling between named pairs, not a new collective variable, and only a 45-day period can resolve it.}

\hcaveats{One period carries the result. The 1-min talk bins put most read-out responses in the same bin as their cause, so the bound misses them and reads low. Naming uses any roster mention, not the leading-@ target. The block-shift null fails against lagged fields, so only the address contrast is a coupling claim. The estimator was fixed after a numerical failure on real behavior data (A2), then re-validated. The power reading of the small periods is post hoc (A3). No read vs unread split of the naming messages.}
