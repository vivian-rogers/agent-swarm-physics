\hwhat{H33 asks whether an agent's daily content diversity has an output optimum. The unit is the agent-day. Its diversity is the participation ratio PR10 of its chat: self near-copies removed, rarefied to 10 statements, whitened bge. Its output is $\log(1+\text{write turns})$. The fit is within agent and period, with day effects and activity controls. The data are 17 non-holdout periods with logged write verbs and 1592 agent-days. The primary test is Simonsohn's two-lines test. A validation on the real design gives 0--7\% false positives and 82\% power for a 0.4-SD inverted U. \textbf{The data show no inverted U.} The slope is $+0.035$ ($p=0.26$) below the breakpoint and $-0.035$ ($p=0.35$) above it. The curve is flat from PR10 $\approx 14$ up. Per period: 0 supported, 3 mixed, 11 failed; the meta-analysed right slope has the wrong sign. PR10 improves day-blocked CV by 0.2\%, and curvature makes it worse. Self-repetition does not predict same-day output ($p=0.84$). \textbf{Round 1b} (work commits, \#30+, both embedding models) keeps the null. The holdout script is frozen, not run.}

\hmodel{Model 11 (vector spins). The degree of freedom is a chat statement: a unit vector in the regime's whitened 32-d bge space. The covariance $\Sigma$ of an agent-day gives the participation ratio PR, the control parameter. PR $=1$ is a frozen spin that repeats itself; PR $=32$ is isotropic (``paramagnetic'') content. H33 claims that output peaks at an intermediate PR. The output is $y_{id}=\log(1+\text{writes})$, with writes = git push or commit, deploy, PR/MR create or merge. $\alpha_{iu}$ and $\delta_d$ are agent$\times$period and day effects; $z$ holds activity controls. $x_c$ is the Robin Hood breakpoint; $\nu=n-1$, $n=10$.}

\hmath{\vspace{-8pt}\begin{gather*}
\mathrm{PR}=(\operatorname{tr}\Sigma)^2/\operatorname{tr}\Sigma^2\ \ (\hat a,\hat b\ \text{unbiased from}\ W=\nu S)\\
y_{id}=\alpha_{iu}+\delta_d+f(\mathrm{PR}_{id})+\gamma^{\!\top} z_{id}+\varepsilon_{id}\\
f=b_1(x-x_c)[x<x_c]+b_2(x-x_c)[x\ge x_c]+c\,[x\ge x_c]\\
\text{inverted U: } b_1>0,\ b_2<0\ (p<.05),\ \arg\max f\ \text{interior}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-10pt}}

\htelos{No content-diversity operating point exists to steer toward. Do not expect more or fewer chat topics to change how much agents ship. Write turns at the best PR10 and at the 90th percentile differ by at most about 9\%. The only hint is on the loop side. The bottom decile may write about 25\% less (not significant). H12's self-repetition rate catches loops more directly. Daily chat PR is half noise, and write turns count activity, not value. This null removes a plausible dashboard metric.}

\hbottom{Agent-day content diversity has no output optimum in write turns or landed commits; curvature hurts out of sample, and the one right-side drop is a \#51 property.}
