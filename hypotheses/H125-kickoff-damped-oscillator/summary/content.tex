\hwhat{HH366 says the kickoff response is an underdamped oscillator: content overshoots the settled goal level on day~1 and swings below it on days 2--3. A fading kickoff field gives the overshoot without the swing. I measured each agent's excess alignment with the kickoff on 27 kickoffs (regimes I--III, 4--21 agents) and compared days 2--3 with days 4--5, against 80 ordinary-day origins. The synthetic check came first: the test has size 0 and power 1 at damping $\zeta\le0.5$. Day~1 overshoots ($E_1=+0.055\pm0.012$, 22/27). Days 2--3 do not undershoot: $U=-0.006\,[-0.020,+0.008]$ (bge), $-0.002\,[-0.017,+0.013]$ (gte), inside the placebo spread. The response is overdamped. Holdout: not run.}

\hmodel{Model 11 with the overdamped relaxation of model 02 as the rival. Spin: agent $i$'s statement vectors in the regime-whitened 32-d basis. Field: the kickoff, direction $\hat k$ from shared \texttt{goal\_fields}. Order parameter: the excess alignment $a=\langle z,\hat k\rangle$ minus the mean over decoy kickoffs, averaged linearly (no renormalized windows). Clock: active hours $h$ since the kickoff (H103). $M_{\rm osc}$: damped oscillator; $M_{\rm fade}$: overdamped spin in a fading field (two exponentials). Both carry a time-of-day profile.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
M_{\rm osc}:\ A(h)=A_\infty+Be^{-\lambda h}\cos(\omega h+\phi),\quad \zeta=\lambda/\sqrt{\lambda^2+\omega^2}\\
M_{\rm fade}:\ A(h)=A_\infty+c_1e^{-h/\tau_1}+c_2e^{-h/\tau_2}\\
U=\big\langle A_{i,4\text{--}5}-A_{i,2\text{--}3}\big\rangle_i,\qquad \zeta_{\rm pk}=\delta/\sqrt{\pi^2+\delta^2},\ \delta=\ln(E_1/U)
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{Read the steady state two active days after a kickoff. Day-1 content is about $0.055$ above the settled alignment and day~2 about $0.02$ above; there is no later dip to wait out. A drop in goal alignment on day~2 or~3 is the kickoff's salience fading, not a loss of the goal. An operator who wants to know where a swarm settled can stop watching after day~3.}

\hbottom{The kickoff response is overdamped: content overshoots the goal on day~1 and settles within two active days without swinging below it.}
