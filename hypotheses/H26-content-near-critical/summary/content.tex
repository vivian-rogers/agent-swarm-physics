\hwhat{HH108 claimed the swarm's \emph{content} is near-critical (H01: $\beta J_0/n=0.74$) while its \emph{activity} is subcritical (loop gains $\le0.4$). We put content, activity and talk on one estimand, $g=1-1/\mathrm{VR}$, at matched resolution (day means, 30-min windows). Field removal was nested: dedupe, goal and kickoff directions, operator-message directions, and a within-room minus cross-room contrast. We ran 10 regime-III two-room units plus \#35 and \#51. Synthetic worlds at village sampling came first. Drives alone reproduce H01's number: 0.57 from a global drive at the observed cross-room level, 0.74 with more drive types. Real data: content's room excess is genuine (median 0.53 [0.34, 0.66]; room-permutation null beaten in 6/10 units). But activity matches it at 30 min (0.44 vs 0.43) and is larger in \#51. Verdicts: 0 supported, 8 mixed, 2 failed. \textbf{Failed as posed.} Holdout script written, not run.}

\hmodel{Mean-field soft-spin O($n$) / Curie--Weiss (models 11, 01). Spins: an agent's 32-d whitened statement mean (content), or its fraction of active or talking minutes. Within-room coupling $J$; drives: global day and time-of-day, static goal fields, measured operator inputs, unmeasured room drives. $C_{ab}$: cross-agent covariance of drive-removed deviations; $S_a$: split-half signal variance; $\rho_w,\rho_c$: within- and cross-room correlation; $N_r$: room size.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
\rho_{w,c}=\frac{\sum_{\rm pairs}\mathrm{tr}\,C_{ab}}{\sum_{\rm pairs}\sqrt{S_aS_b}},\qquad
\rho_{\rm ex}=\frac{\rho_w-\rho_c}{1-\rho_c}\\
g=1-\frac{1}{1+(N_r-1)\,\rho_{\rm ex}},\qquad g_{\rm eq}=J,\quad g_{\rm day}\to J(2-J)
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{Mostly a correction, with one usable diagnostic. The HH108 gap was an artifact: it set a day-mean statistic that includes drives against a 1-min statistic with drives removed. Time-averaging alone turns an equal-time gain of 0.4 into 0.64. Compare channels at the same resolution and with the same drive removal and the gap disappears: ideas do not cascade more easily than effort. The diagnostic is \emph{where} co-movement lives. Content co-moves within rooms (cross-room $\rho_c\approx0.05$). Activity co-moves village-wide ($\rho_c$ 0.5--0.8), so a swarm-wide activity dip is a platform or schedule event. Operator messages explain none of the room content co-movement. Near-critical is too strong for either channel: room loop gains of about 0.5 mean variance about twice the independent level.}

\hbottom{At matched time resolution and drive removal, LLM-swarm content and activity have similar room loop gains ($\approx0.5$); H01's 0.74 is mostly drives plus time-averaging, and the channels differ in \emph{where} they co-move, not how strongly.}
