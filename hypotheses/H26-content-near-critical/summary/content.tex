\hwhat{HH108 claimed that the swarm's \emph{content} is near-critical (H01: $\beta J_0/n=0.74$) while its \emph{activity} is subcritical (loop gains $\le0.4$). We put content, activity and talk on one estimand, $g=1-1/\mathrm{VR}$, at matched resolution (day means, 30-min windows). Field removal is nested and ends in a within-room minus cross-room contrast. We run 10 regime-III two-room units plus \#35 and \#51. In synthetic worlds, drives alone reproduce H01's number (0.57 from a global drive, 0.74 with more drive types). Round 1: content's room excess is genuine (median 0.53; null beaten in 6/10 units). Activity matches it (0.44 vs 0.43 at 30 min): \textbf{failed as posed}. \textbf{Round 1b} (fixed activity table, two embedding models): activity's room gain collapses to 0.12 at 30 min. Its cross-room $\rho$ falls 0.50 $\to$ 0.10, the event-drop artifact. Content now exceeds activity (gap +0.41 [0.13, ...], 9/10 units). Talk matches content, and content stays $\approx$0.5: \textbf{unresolved}. NE42: the room excess is channel-borne. The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Mean-field O($n$) soft spins and Curie--Weiss (11, 01). Spins: an agent's 32-d whitened statement mean (content), or its fraction of active or talking minutes. Coupling $J$ acts within a room. Drives: global day and time-of-day, static goal fields, measured operator inputs and unmeasured room drives. $C_{ab}$: cross-agent covariance of drive-removed deviations. $S_a$: split-half signal variance. $\rho_w,\rho_c$: within- and cross-room correlation. $N_r$: room size. Order parameter: the room-excess loop gain $g$.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
\rho_{w,c}=\frac{\sum_{\rm pairs}\mathrm{tr}\,C_{ab}}{\sum_{\rm pairs}\sqrt{S_aS_b}},\qquad
\rho_{\rm ex}=\frac{\rho_w-\rho_c}{1-\rho_c}\\
g=1-\frac{1}{1+(N_r-1)\,\rho_{\rm ex}},\qquad g_{\rm eq}=J,\quad g_{\rm day}\to J(2-J)
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\hcaveats{Unmeasured room drives read as coupling: forks in \#35, instructions in \#38, the override in \#44. Day-level activity is nearly unpowered (3--8 days), and the split-half variance of talk is unreliable. In synthetic worlds the estimator favors content, which strengthens the null. One-room units lack a baseline. Both embedding models agree, and the trimmed activity variant keeps the 30-min gap (+0.22 [0.01, ...]). Goals confound NE42, no cascade sizes exist yet (H34), and \#51 confounds the post hoc size scaling.}
