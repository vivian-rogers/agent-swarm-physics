\hwhat{The claim: coordinated agents plus their artifacts form \emph{effective superagents}. Such a unit predicts its members' next artifact better than their own histories do. The unit state records \emph{which} repo each member advances per 30-min bin. Candidates come coordination-first: multi-layer communities, crews and a calibrated subset search. \textbf{Agent + own artifact} is the null unit. The method has power 0.60--0.75 in \#51b--d (H01's binary state: $\le$5\%). Tests: G51, G38 and the natives NE42, \#44, \#focus. \textbf{No unit beats the null where the method has power.} The one qualifier (\#51e) is three agents that avoid each other's repos. After forced erasures, members return to their \emph{own} artifact. Single agents with their own artifacts carry organismal $A^*$ of 0.05--0.28 bits per bin (\#38, \#51). Crews have low colonial $A$ relative to size-matched groups, high environmental coding (NTIC $=A^*-A$) and large environmental determination $nC$. The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Semantic information (model 04) with Krakauer individuality. The member state $s_i(b)$ is the repo $i$ commits to in bin $b$. $\ell_i$ is its last own repo, carried across erasures and nights; $o_i$ is the other members' repo. $\tilde\pi_i$ and $\tilde\pi'_i$ are $i$'s repo popularity $\pi_i$ renormalized without $\ell_i$ (and $o_i$). The null $M_0$ stays on $\ell_i$ or moves by $\pi_i$; $M_1$ adds joining $o_i$. The coordination gain $g$ is a held-out transfer entropy about \emph{which} artifact. $A_G$ is colonial individuality.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
p_0(k)=w_s\,\delta_{k\ell}+(1-w_s)\,\tilde\pi_i(k)\\
p_1(k)=w_s\,\delta_{k\ell}+(1-w_s)\big[v_j\,\delta_{ko}+(1-v_j)\,\tilde\pi'_i(k)\big]\\
g_G=\big\langle \log_2 p_1(s_i')-\log_2 p_0(s_i')\big\rangle_{\text{held-out days}}\\
A_G=I(x';x\,|\,y),\qquad \iota_G=A_G/H(x')
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{Treat one agent and its own repository as the operational unit, not the team. Keep each agent's work state legible in its own repo. After a context erasure, an agent re-reads its own artifact before its first commit in 50\% of cases (placebo 33\%). It rarely moves to the group's artifact (1.1\%). Even a team with a dedicated shared repo (\#44 \#best) works in its members' own repos. Co-allocation on popular artifacts exists, but outsiders on those repos co-move as much: it behaves as a shared field (goal or popularity). To steer co-allocation, change the field; a team channel does not do it. This sharpens H01's negative, now with power where it matters.}

\hbottom{No coordinated superagent: with a which-artifact state and real power, agent + own artifact stays the persistent unit, and group co-allocation is a shared field.}

\hcaveats{Power exists only in \#51b--d; 5-day units and the \#40 and \#44 natives miss stores of $\rho\lesssim0.5$. The gain $g$ rewards avoidance as well as attraction (found post hoc, A3). Artifact = repo, so division of labour inside a repo shows only at \#40's file level. Commits are attempts. Erasure outcome windows end at the next reset; the conditional estimate is primary. Reads are artifact mentions in executed commands. The environment $y$ omits the scheduler, kickoff and prior fields. The normalization $\iota$ and the z-scores are project choices, not the paper's. Replication covers G51 and G38 only (compute). Only natural scrambles exist; the artifact-store scramble (NE24) is in the holdout.}
