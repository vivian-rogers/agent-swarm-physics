\hwhat{Built a reusable daily dial: the Curie--Weiss loop gain $g=1-1/\mathrm{VR}$ and $T/T_c=1/g$, per day and channel, from logs, with stalls masked, slow drives removed and calibrated 90\% intervals; ran it on 282 non-holdout days. \textbf{Round 1b} re-ran it on the corrected activity table (round 1 had lost half of all events) with DQ8's calibrated null. \textbf{Activity and talk are subcritical on every day} (median $g=0.16$ both, was 0.10 and 0.12; $T/T_c\approx6$); trimmed to the common running window, activity drops to 0.07 and beats the independent-agent ceiling on only 24\% of days. The dial now reproduces H19 on the same table ($\rho$ 0.94, 0.97) and tracks H03's Hawkes $\hat n$ ($\rho=0.46$). \textbf{Content still reads near-critical} (0.70; P1 failed), as swarm size times a shared-field correlation. Native tests: withdrawing the operator's drives (NE43) leaves the dial unchanged; talk co-activation vanishes when 15 agents share one room (NE42). Holdout not run.}

\hmodel{Models 01 (Curie--Weiss), 09 (Hawkes), 11 (O($n$) spins). $s_i=\pm1$: agent $i$ active, or posting, in a minute; for content, $s_i$ is its 30-min mean of whitened unit message embeddings ($n=32$). $h_i(t)$: private fields (schedule, goal), removed by 30-min block means and agent-day means; stalls masked. $q_i=1-m_i^2$; $g$: loop gain; VR: collective over independent variance; $\bar\rho$: mean pairwise correlation; $N$: agents that day.}

\hmath{\vspace{-8pt}\begin{gather*}
H=-\tfrac{J_0}{2N}\Big(\textstyle\sum_i s_i\Big)^2-\sum_i h_i(t)\,s_i,\qquad g=\beta J_0\,\bar q\\
\delta M=\frac{\sum_j q_j\beta\,\delta h_j}{1-g},\qquad \mathrm{VR}=\frac{\operatorname{Var}\sum_i s_i}{\sum_i\operatorname{Var}s_i}=\frac{1}{1-g}\\
g=1-\mathrm{VR}^{-1},\qquad T/T_c=1/g\ \ (h=0)\\
\mathrm{VR}-1=(N-1)\,\bar\rho\ \Rightarrow\ g(N)=\frac{(N-1)\bar\rho}{1+(N-1)\bar\rho}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-10pt}}

\htelos{The useful part is a calibrated instrument and three warnings. Any operator can compute the dial from event logs with \texttt{compute\_dial}; here it says the swarm amplifies a nudge by 10--20\%: no fast runaway. Warning 1: a green dial does not rule out slow herding through context read minutes later. Warning 2: $g$ rises with headcount at fixed per-pair correlation, so track $\bar\rho$, weekly. Warning 3 (new): trim each day to the common running window before reading activity feedback; without it, half the ``signal'' is agents booting together. The talk dial is now the credible gauge: it matches an independent Hawkes fit and survives trimming.}

\hbottom{A daily Curie--Weiss dial is computable from logs with honest intervals and, on corrected data, agrees with H19 and H03 and shows activity and talk far from criticality ($g\approx0.16$, activity 0.07 after trimming), but it is blind to delayed coupling and its near-critical content reading is size times a shared field.}
