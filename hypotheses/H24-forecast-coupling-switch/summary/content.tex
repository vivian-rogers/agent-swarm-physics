\hwhat{Goal period \#21 (AI forecasts; regime I; 8--9 agents) serves as a natural experiment. The record dates the first reading of a teammate's forecast at 42--86 min into the week (median 50 min), not mid-week. There, the share of messages that name a teammate jumps from 7\% to 74\%. The predicted alignment step is absent: $\Delta A_{\rm res}=+0.010$ [$-0.05$, $+0.09$], inside a within-week placebo and 23 other kickoff days. Content was aligned before any reading ($A_{\rm res}\approx0.33$ vs a rotation null of 0.03). Numeric herding was an extraction artifact (hand-verified $\kappa=0.25$, $p=0.17$). \textbf{Round 1b} adds a second embedding model, shared and multi-direction goal fields, dedupe and style residuals. The step fails in all 13 configurations. gte sees $+0.16$, but ordinary kickoff days match it. Natives: reading a teammate's document pulls the reader toward it by $\approx0.03$. One kickoff split into two rooms grew two room cultures. The holdout (\#14) is not run.}

\hmodel{Vector spins (11) with a Curie--Weiss analogue (01). Spin $\mathbf s_i$: the whitened, unit-normalized statement embedding of agent $i$ ($n=32$). Field $h\hat g$: the goal and kickoff text. $\mathbf h_i$: agent style. Coupling $J_0$: a mean-field pull toward the others, switched on at $\tau_i$, the first reading of a teammate's forecast by agent $i$. Order parameter $A$: mean pairwise cosine; $A_{\rm res}$ removes $\hat g$. Numeric forecasts $x_i$ (AGI by 2035, SI by 2050, p(doom)) are soft spins with a DeGroot pull $\kappa$.}
\hmath{\vspace{-8pt}
\begin{gather*}
\mathbf s_i(t)\propto h\,\hat g+\mathbf h_i+\boldsymbol\xi_i(t)+J_0\,\Theta(t-\tau_i)\,\mathbf m_{-i}(t)\\
A=\tfrac{2}{N(N-1)}\textstyle\sum_{i<j}\hat{\mathbf v}_i\cdot\hat{\mathbf v}_j,\qquad \beta J_0/n=1-\dfrac{1}{1+(N-1)A_{\rm res}}\\
\Delta x_i=\kappa\,(\bar x_{-i}-x_i)+\epsilon_i,\qquad \kappa=0\ \text{for independent updating}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-6pt}}

\htelos{An ``independent drafts first'' phase does not protect the diversity of swarm forecasts. Content was aligned before any agent read another ($A_{\rm res}\approx0.33$), from the shared prompt, instructions and prior. Measure diversity at kickoff, not after drafting. Comparison then produced talk about divergence and some literal copying of numbers, not an alignment jump. Do not measure herding on regex-extracted numbers: extraction errors make false herding through regression to the mean. One week with $N=7$ and no ground truth makes this a pilot.}
\hbottom{The forecast-week coupling switch moved swarm alignment no more than ordinary kickoff days; coupling is pairwise, about 0.03 in cosine per document read.}

\hcaveats{The test bed is one forecasting week with $N=7$. The switch-on and the reading events rest on the agents' own screen descriptions. The step statistic depends on the embedding model. The pair DiD weakens after style residualization, so part of it may be register. In \#41 everyone reads room-mates within a minute, so early coupling and room composition are not separable. \#41 has only 9 one-statement drafts.}
