\hwhat{Goal period \#21 (forecast AI; regime I; 8--9 agents) as a natural experiment. From the record, each agent first read a teammate's forecast 42--86 min into the week (median 50 min), not mid-week; the share of messages naming a teammate jumps there from 7\% to 74\%. The predicted alignment step did not appear: $\Delta A_{\rm res}=+0.010$ [$-0.05$, $+0.09$], inside a within-week placebo and 23 other kickoff days. Content was aligned before any reading ($A_{\rm res}\approx0.33$ vs.\ a rotation null of 0.03). Numeric herding was an extraction artifact (hand-verified $\kappa=0.25$, $p=0.17$). \textbf{Round 1b} (second embedding model, shared and multi-direction goal field, dedupe, style): the step fails in all 13 configurations, but gte sees $+0.16$ that ordinary kickoff days match. Natives: reading a teammate's document pulls the reader toward it by $\approx0.03$; one kickoff split into two rooms grew two room cultures. Holdout (\#14) not run.}

\hmodel{Model 11 (vector spins), with model 01's Curie--Weiss analogue. Spin $\mathbf s_i$: agent $i$'s whitened, unit-normalized statement embedding ($n=32$). Field $h\hat g$: the goal and kickoff text. $\mathbf h_i$: agent style. Coupling $J_0$: a mean-field pull toward the others, switched on at $\tau_i$ (the agent's first reading of a teammate's forecast). $A$: mean pairwise cosine, with $\hat g$ removed for $A_{\rm res}$. Numeric forecasts $x_i$ (AGI by 2035, SI by 2050, p(doom)) are soft spins with a DeGroot pull $\kappa$.}

\end{gather*}\vspace{-6pt}}

\htelos{Mostly a cautionary tale. Telling LLM agents to ``form independent views, then compare'' did not buy independence: their content was aligned before anyone read anyone, from the shared prompt, instructions and prior. Comparison then produced talk about divergence, and some literal copying of numbers, not a measurable alignment jump. For anyone aggregating swarm forecasts, an independent-first-draft phase is no diversity guarantee: most of the diversity worth protecting is gone at kickoff. The reusable lessons are methodological. Regex-extracted numbers manufacture herding through regression to the mean, and snapshot coupling estimates absorb unmodelled fields. One week, $N=7$, no ground truth: a pilot.}
\hbottom{In the forecast week the coupling switch was real but early and moved no swarm-level alignment beyond ordinary kickoff days, in either embedding model; the coupling that exists is pairwise and gated by reading, about 0.03 in cosine per document read.}
