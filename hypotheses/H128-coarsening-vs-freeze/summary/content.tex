\hwhat{HH369 says that a goal kickoff is a zero-temperature Potts quench on the agents' work projects. A free kickoff should coarsen: projects merge and their number falls as $t^{-\alpha}$ with $\alpha\approx0.3$--0.5. A kickoff that names its target should freeze at once. H128 replays every agent's work-ledger host label after 12 non-holdout kickoffs (7 free, 5 named; regimes I--III) and two natives (\#44 rooms, NE42). \textbf{Free and named kickoffs give the same curves}: freeze time $p=0.32$, excess area $p=0.73$, where a true coarsening-vs-freeze world gives $p<0.05$ with power 0.96--1.00. Free weeks lose projects by finishing, not merging (merge share 0.00--0.38 in 7/7). HH369's kill is met. The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Model 10, kinetic Potts quench with births. At each of its work windows an agent picks repo $j$ with $P(j)\propto e^{\beta J s_j+h_j+b\,[j=\sigma_i]}$ plus a new-repo option. Free kickoff: $h=0$, so domains merge by coupling (mean-field voter: $N_p\propto1/t$). Named kickoff: $h_T\gg1$ on a shared target or one target per agent, so the count jumps to its final value in one round. Rival R1: projects end by finishing (expiry), with no merging.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
d_w(t)=\frac{N_p(t)-1}{N_h(t)-1},\qquad t_f=\min\{t:\ |d_w(s)-d_w^\infty|\le\delta\ \forall s\ge t\},\quad \delta=\max\!\Big(\tfrac{1}{\bar N_h-1},\,0.15\,d_w^\infty\Big)\\
A_u=\int_{t_\mathrm{pk}}^{t_\mathrm{pk}+8\,\mathrm{h}}\frac{d_w(t)-d_w^\infty}{d_w^\mathrm{pk}-d_w^\infty}\,dt,\qquad c_m=\frac{\#\,\text{merge deaths}}{\#\,\text{project deaths}}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator: naming the target in a kickoff does not make the swarm converge on fewer projects. Own-artifact goals freeze at one project per agent from the first hour. A free goal leaves agents scattered or splitting, and projects end when work stops, not by merging (merge share $\le0.38$). To get agents onto one repo, give a shared target to a swarm that is already scattered (NE42: $d_w$ 1 to 0.2 over about 16 active hours). Expect hours, not minutes.}

\hbottom{After a goal kickoff, the spread of agents over work projects does not separate free from named kickoffs (freeze time $p=0.32$, excess area $p=0.73$, power $\ge0.96$), and free weeks lose projects by finishing, not merging (merge share 0.00--0.38): HH369's coarsening-vs-freeze split is killed.}
