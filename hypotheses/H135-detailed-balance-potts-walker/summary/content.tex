\hwhat{HH378 says that H94's max-ent allocation is the steady state of a reversible kinetic Potts walker, so the hop-rate ratio between two projects equals their max-ent occupancy ratio. H135 rebuilds every agent's project hops (work host labels; attention labels) and refits H94's max-ent occupancies $\pi$ per period unit on non-reserved data, then tests zero net flux between projects that live through the unit and the heat-bath destination law. \textbf{The round is inconclusive by the card's rule.} The strict co-alive rule leaves 0/62 unit-channels testable, and the zero-flux test has no power against the rival walkers. On the five \#51 attention units testable under the card's 80\% variant, \textbf{destinations are not heat-bath}: $\psi=0.44$ [0.41, 0.48] instead of 1, with a habit bonus $\rho=1.9$ nats.}

\hmodel{Models 10 and 15. Agent $i$ walks on the available projects with field $h_i(j)=\ln\pi_i(j)$, $\pi_i$ the agent-conditional of H94's max-ent fit (margins, ownership, rooms). At each own call it updates with an aging hazard; the heat-bath update draws the new project from $\pi_i$. Detailed balance then gives zero net flux on every pair and destinations $\propto\pi_i$. Rivals: age drift, herding sink, cycle force, habit.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
\frac{k_{ab}}{k_{ba}}=\frac{n_{ab}}{n_{ba}}\frac{T_b}{T_a}\overset{\text{DB}}{=}\frac{\pi_b}{\pi_a},\qquad m_\pi=\frac{\sum_{\text{pairs}}(n_{ab}-n_{ba})\,\mathrm{sgn}\ln(\pi_b/\pi_a)}{\sum(n_{ab}+n_{ba})}\\
P(b\mid\text{hop from }a)\propto\exp\!\big[\psi\ln\pi_i(b)+\rho\,[b\text{ held}]\big],\qquad \text{heat-bath: }\psi=1,\ \rho=0
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator: the max-ent prices (ownership, rooms) set where agents spend their time, but they do not set where a moving agent goes next. A hopping agent responds to a project's max-ent weight with less than half the heat-bath exponent and prefers projects it has held before by about $e^{1.9}\approx7\times$. To redirect agents, name the target explicitly; raising its general attractiveness moves hop destinations only weakly.}
