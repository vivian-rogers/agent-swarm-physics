\hwhat{H36 asks whether fluctuation statistics (multi-information, susceptibility, a heat-capacity analogue) flag swarm reorganizations. Rounds 1 and 1b: the physics alarm catches a quarter of 33 goal changes, all via content; the topic shift R1 scores AUC 0.95. \textbf{Round 2} (non-reserved data). (i) \textbf{Intraday}: a 30-min topic-shift alarm fires in the first window of day~0 for 82--85\% of kickoffs, against 7--9\% of placebo day starts (AUC 0.92--0.94). (ii) \textbf{Robustness}: the frozen rule C3 (R1~$\ge$~3 or $Z_{\rm cont}\ge2$) keeps hit 0.55--0.64 at window FAR 0.02--0.07 in 6 embedding~$\times$~dedupe variants and on Monday-matched placebos. The gte FAR moves with the surrogate seed (0.07--0.18). (iii) Sampling-invariant activity statistics still react to roster and schedule changes. (iv) An action-mix scaffold detector fails (AUC 0.61, $p=0.16$). The physics alarm fails random dates in every variant ($p$ 0.12--0.25).}
\hmodel{Models 01 (inverse Ising) and 11 (vector spins). The activity spin $s_i(t)=\pm1$ marks agent $i$ active in minute $t$; $M=\sum_i s_i$; VR is the susceptibility as a variance ratio. The content spin $\hat{\mathbf s}_i(w)$ is agent $i$'s unit content vector in window $w$; $\bar{\mathbf m}_w$ is the swarm mean of the centered vectors. $D(w)$ is the intraday topic shift against the previous 4 windows; $z$ scores are trailing robust z (10 days, or 16 windows).}
\hmath{\vspace{-8pt}
\begin{gather*}
\mathrm{VR}=\frac{\mathrm{Var}_t M}{\sum_i \mathrm{Var}_t s_i},\qquad I_G=-\tfrac12\log\det R,\qquad Z_{\rm phys}=\big\langle Z_I,Z_\chi,Z_C\big\rangle\ \ge 2\\
D(w)=1-\cos\!\big(\bar{\mathbf m}_w,\ \langle\bar{\mathbf m}\rangle_{w-4..w-1}\big),\qquad \text{alarm: } z_{16}[D(w)]\ge 3\\
\mathrm{C3}:\ z_{10}[R_1(d)]\ge3\ \ \text{or}\ \ Z_{\rm cont}(d)\ge2\ \ \text{on } d\in\{-1,0,1\}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-6pt}}
