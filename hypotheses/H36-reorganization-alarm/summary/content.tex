\hwhat{H36 computes three fluctuation statistics for each of 279 non-holdout days: multi-information, susceptibility and a heat-capacity analogue. Each uses 1-min activity spins, 4-state behavior and 30-min content vectors, as an excess over shuffled surrogates. The pre-registered alarm fires when the trailing z-score reaches 2. \textbf{The result is mixed.} The alarm catches 10 of 33 goal changes within a day, at a 10\% placebo-window false-alarm rate (AUC 0.68, random-date $p=0.03$). All of the signal is content: activity is at chance once roster and day-length changes are out, and $\sim$40\% of the content signal is within-day drift. The plain day-to-day shift of the mean content vector scores AUC 0.95. The alarm catches 1/13 scaffold changes. \textbf{Round 1b} uses a fixed activity table, removes restatements and adds a second embedding model. Hits fall to 0.21--0.27, and the random-date $p$ rises to 0.08 (bge) and 0.21 (gte), at the failure boundary. Content (AUC 0.74--0.78) and topic shift (0.95--0.97) replicate. NE40 and NE45 go unseen. The holdout is not run.}
\hmodel{Models 01 (inverse Ising) and 11 (vector spins). The activity spin $s_i(t)=\pm1$ marks agent $i$ active in minute $t$; $M=\sum_i s_i$. The content spin $\hat{\mathbf s}_i(w)$ is agent $i$'s whitened unit content vector in window $w$. $\mathrm{VR}$ is the susceptibility as a variance ratio (1 for independent agents). $c$ is a heat-capacity analogue with alignment energy $E$. $I_G$ is the Gaussian multi-information ($R$: correlation matrix); $e(w)$ is the content alignment energy. Each statistic enters as observed minus surrogate mean, then as a trailing 10-day z.}
\hmath{\vspace{-8pt}
\begin{gather*}
\mathrm{VR}=\frac{\mathrm{Var}_t M}{\sum_i \mathrm{Var}_t s_i},\qquad c=\frac{\mathrm{Var}_t E}{n},\quad E=-\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i<j}s_is_j\\
I_G=-\tfrac12\log\det R,\qquad e(w)=-\big\langle \hat{\mathbf s}_i(w)\cdot\hat{\mathbf s}_j(w)\big\rangle_{i<j}\\
z_X(d)=\frac{X(d)-\mathrm{med}_{10}X}{\mathrm{sd}^{\rm trim}_{10}X/c_{10}},\qquad Z_{\rm phys}=\big\langle Z_I,Z_\chi,Z_C\big\rangle\ \ge 2
\end{gather*}\vspace{-6pt}}

\htelos{To flag a swarm reorganization, use a first-moment detector: the day-to-day shift of the mean content vector (AUC 0.95). Fluctuation statistics barely respond. Activity fluctuations follow who is present and for how long; content fluctuations follow the within-day settling after a new goal. Pending the holdout, the content-fluctuation score adds one use: a false-alarm filter beside the topic-shift detector. In round 1 the pair keeps the shift detector's hit rate at about a tenth of the false alarms. Scaffold changes, the events an operator most wants flagged, stay invisible to all of these daily statistics.}
\hbottom{Multi-information and susceptibility barely mark reorganizations: on corrected data the alarm catches a quarter of goal changes, all via content, and a topic-shift detector does far better.}
