\hwhat{H61 asks if the fitness of a new idea is visible at its first use. An idea is a hashed H34 marker: a link, a number, a name or a rare word. Its reach is the number of agents who use it within 24\,h. The forecast uses seed features: the marker class, the seed message's length and novel load, its distance from the kickoff field, the poster's reply in-degree, the receptive fraction, and whether the message is addressed or threaded. Models are fitted on earlier days and scored on later days, in 27 eligible non-holdout periods (99,695 held-out ideas). The seed features beat the strongest baseline (class + poster + kickoff + room size) in only 4/27 periods. The pooled gain is $+0.1\pm1.6$ millinats per idea. The gain is clear only in the two largest regime-III swarms. \textbf{Mostly refuted.} The holdout is untouched.}

\hmodel{Model 03 (subcritical branching) with idea-level fitness. Idea $i$ has its own reproduction number $R_i$. Its log is a sum of a period term, a class term, a poster term, the seed-feature term $\theta\cdot x_i$, and unobserved noise $\eta_i$. For small $R_i$, the chance of reaching a second agent is $1-e^{-R_i}$, so a logistic model in $x_i$ is the first-order fitness model. H61 holds if $\theta$ adds held-out skill beyond the class and poster terms. A rival (R4) is co-generation: features that predict ideas many agents produce at once from a common source.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
\log R_i=\alpha_g+\beta_{{\rm class}(i)}+\gamma_{{\rm poster}(i)}+\theta\cdot x_i+\eta_i,\qquad P(S_i\ge2)\simeq 1-e^{-R_i}\\
\Delta{\rm LL}=\tfrac{1}{n}\textstyle\sum_{i\in\text{test}}\big[\ell_F(y_i)-\ell_{B4}(y_i)\big],\quad \text{train: days}<d,\ \text{test: day } d
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{To get an item picked up, post it alone, in a short message, as a reply. In \#51 the seed length costs $-0.24$ log-odds per SD of length, and each additional coined item costs $-0.13$. Do not rank ideas or agents by reply in-degree or by who is about to read: neither predicts spread ($+0.04\pm0.04$, $+0.00\pm0.02$). A first-use forecast works only in a large regime-III swarm (top-decile lift 2.5 in \#51). In small swarms, rank by who posted it.}

\hbottom{An idea's spread is mostly not visible at first use beyond who posted it; the replicated signal is message-level dilution: long, item-dense seeds spread each item less.}

\hcaveats{Ideas are hashed markers, so long reports and code dumps coin many low-value names; length and novel load partly measure that. Reach counts co-generated uses. The in-flight placebo lacks power, because unread adopters are about 1\% of adopters. Coefficients are in-sample; only $\Delta$LL, AUC and lift are held out. The 27 replication folders are one estimator, not 27 tests. A1 and A2 were written after the synthetic runs and before any real outcome was modelled. Some working timestamps were first written ahead of the real clock and were corrected from file times.}
