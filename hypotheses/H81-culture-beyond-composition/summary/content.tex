\hwhat{The test asks whether village content carries a slow component that its members do not explain. The null is each member's personal vector (two-way fixed effects on its other goal periods) plus the measured goal fields. In regime I (24 goals) culture residuals of blocks $\le14$ days apart align ($\bar s=0.17$), decaying with $\tau_u\approx23$--28 days; the contrast is 3 times the synthetic null in both embedding models and survives turnover. Round 2 asked what carries it. It does not follow the long-lived artifact record its members read ($C_{R1}=+0.02/-0.07$ against an exogenous-drift 95th percentile of 0.11/0.09, power 1.0). It survives removing operator directions beyond the centroid and is not concentrated in outside-world talk. H82's boundary trace fits the same $\tau$. Calendar versus village time cannot be identified, so outside drift is narrowed, not beaten.}

\hmodel{Vector spins (model 11). The spin $v_{i,d}$ is agent $i$'s style-residualized content vector on day $d$ (32-d). $a_i$ is the personal vector (weights, family, style). $h_G$ is the exogenous field of goal period $G$, removed by projection $\Pi_G$. $w_b$ is a topic field with no memory. $u_b$ is the collective slow mode, an Ornstein--Uhlenbeck vector with correlation time $\tau_u$. The order parameter is the block culture residual $u_b$. The statistic $D$ is the near-minus-far similarity of $u_b$ across goal boundaries, adjusted for goal similarity ($D_{\rm adjg}$) and member overlap ($D_{\rm adj}$).}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
v_{i,d}=a_i+h_{G(d)}+w_{b(d)}+u_{b(d)}+e_{i,d},\qquad \langle u_b\cdot u_{b'}\rangle\propto e^{-|t_b-t_{b'}|/\tau_u}\\
r_{i,b}=\Pi_G\bigl(\bar v_{i,b}-\hat a_i^{(-G)}\bigr),\qquad u_b=\langle r_{i,b}\rangle_{i\in b}\\
D=\langle\cos(u_b,u_{b'})\rangle_{\Delta t\le14\,\mathrm d}-\langle\cos(u_b,u_{b'})\rangle_{\Delta t\ge42\,\mathrm d}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{Expect a village to drift as a whole on a timescale of about a month, independent of who is in it. In regime I the slow-mode contrast matches a synthetic mode that carries half of the period-field variance ($s=0.5$). That share is inferred from the match, not measured. A new kickoff does not reset the village: neighbouring goals share about 0.17 of their residual direction. Roster changes do not move the mode. Its carrier is still unknown, but it is not the long-lived artifact record (old sites, documents, repos), the operator's messages or outside-world talk, so curating those artifacts will not steer it. For monitoring, compare a village with its own last two to three weeks, not with its whole history.}

\hbottom{In regime I the village content has a collective slow mode ($\tau_u\approx25$ days) beyond its members and the measured goal and operator fields, carried across turnover; it does not follow the long-lived artifact record its members read, and an unmeasured calendar-time drift remains the open rival.}
