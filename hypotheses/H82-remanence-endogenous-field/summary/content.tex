\hwhat{The test decomposes each agent's day-1 content in a new goal period into an exogenous field (new kickoff, goal text, previous kickoff, operator messages), the agent's prior from other periods, and the previous period's village centroid. It runs on 25 goal boundaries with both embedding models. The previous centroid loads by $\Delta\gamma_1\approx0.15$ beyond placebo centroids, 3 times the synthetic null. The trace does not decay over five days. The next period's centroid also loads, at about half the size. With the agent's own previous content as a regressor, the village centroid adds nothing ($-0.02$ / $-0.06$). Newcomers are too few to test. Read previous content predicts day-1 content better than unread content in one model. The holdout script exists and is not run.}

\hmodel{Vector spins (model 11) with a two-part mean field. The spin $v_{i,d}$ is the agent-day content vector (32-d, style-residualized). $X_{\rm ex}$ are the exogenous directions. $p_i$ is the agent's prior, fitted without periods $P$ and $P-1$. $e_{P-1}^{(-i)}$ is the previous village centroid without agent $i$. Its loading $\gamma$ is the endogenous field. The quench (H54) is the loading $\alpha$ on the new kickoff. Hysteresis is $\gamma>0$ with $\gamma[P-1]>\gamma[P+1]$. A record-borne trace would also load in newcomers.}

\hmath{\vspace{-6pt}\begin{gather*}
v_{i,d}=\textstyle\sum_k\alpha_k X_{\mathrm{ex},k}+\beta\,p_i^{(-P,-X)}+\gamma_d\,e_X^{(-i)}+\varepsilon_{i,d}\\
\Delta\gamma_d=\gamma_d[X{=}P{-}1]-\mathrm{median}_{|Q-P|\ge2}\,\gamma_d[X{=}Q],\qquad A=\gamma_1[P{-}1]-\gamma_1[P{+}1]
\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

Regime-I boundaries rest on 3--4 agents. (b) Random-effects mean of $\Delta\gamma_d$ minus the null bias over the first five active days. There is no decay.}

\htelos{A goal change re-points agents onto the new kickoff, but each veteran keeps its own previous themes for at least five days. That carry-over is about 0.15 in loading units, beyond the new fields and the agent's prior. For an operator: a new goal does not erase the old one, so expect about a week of mixed content. Retire or reset a specific agent's context if its old thread must stop. Nothing here shows that the village record carries the trace on its own. The trace follows members and partly what they read.}

\hbottom{The previous goal leaves a persistent trace on day-1 content ($\Delta\gamma_1\approx0.15$, 25 boundaries), but it is each agent's own carry-over, not a decaying remanence of the village record.}
