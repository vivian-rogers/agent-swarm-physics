\hwhat{HH339 says that a forced context erasure (NE41, the 41-call cap) is a demagnetizing pulse. If a room's content order is held by coupling through the context window, an agent's alignment with its room's direction should drop right after an erasure and recover as it re-reads the room. H109 projects every statement on the leave-agent-out spontaneous room axis (H100's $\Delta_\mathrm{spont}$) in 8 non-holdout regime-III two-room periods and compares 3,477 forced erasures with gap-matched within-segment boundaries of the same agent and day. \textbf{There is no pulse.} Pooled $\delta_F=-0.08$ [$-0.23$, 0.07] (gte $-0.07$), where a 30\% drop is detected with power 1.00. HH339's kill fires. The holdout is not run.}

\hmodel{Model 11 (soft-spin O(32) in a two-domain room system) with the erasure as a natural scramble of one channel (model 04). A statement is $\tilde{\mathbf{x}}_t=\mathbf{a}_i+\sigma_r m(t)\,\mathbf{u}_P+\boldsymbol\eta_t$. Context-held order: $m$ drops by $\delta$ after an erasure and recovers with the room items $R$ read. Self-made field (own work, prompt, memory): $\delta=0$. The kickoff, held in the prompt since NE13, must not move in either case.}

a_t=\sigma_{r(t)}\,\langle\tilde{\mathbf{x}}_t-\hat{\mathbf{a}}_i,\ \hat{\mathbf{u}}_i\rangle,\qquad m(R)=m_\infty\big[1-\delta\,e^{-R/R_0}\big]\\

\end{gather*}\vspace{-8pt}}

\htelos{For an operator: resetting an agent's context does not erase its room's content identity. Alignment with the room stays within $-7\%$ to $+23\%$ of its pre-erasure level (pooled 95\% CI). Use erasures to break loops or clear clutter (H44, H69) without worrying about room differentiation. To change what a room talks about, change its work or instructions. Reading the room has only a small pull ($\kappa_R\approx0.02$ per e-fold of items read).}

\hbottom{A forced context erasure does not demagnetize an agent's room alignment ($\delta_F=-0.08$ [$-0.23$, 0.07], power 1.00 at a 30\% drop); room order lives in the agent's work, prompt and memory, not in its context window.}

\hcaveats{Scoped statements are sparse (median 2 per reset segment), so effect sizes are read on the estimand scale (Amendment 1); HH339's literal ``30\% for 3 statements'' world has an estimand of only 0.12. Per-period power is $\ge0.8$ only in \#38 and 51g; six per-period negatives are inconclusive. The room axis is a period-level ruler; a faster, other-direction room component would be missed. A drop that recovers before the first post-erasure statement is invisible. The post-erasure rise ($-0.12$ [$-0.21$, $-0.03$] direct pooled) is post hoc.}
