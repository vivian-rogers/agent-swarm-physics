\hwhat{The data is 15 counted non-reserved units (\#35--\#44, \#51; $N=10$--27). The state is each agent's daily content vector with the day's common field removed. Same-lab agents share a content direction in 9/15 units (RE $T=0.08$ [0.02, 0.14]), stable across periods. \textbf{Round 2} grades the style rival. A within-agent map of 20 formatting features and 50 function words keeps \textbf{32\% (bge) to 47\% (gte)} of the field: $T=0.026$ [$-$0.01, 0.06] and 0.040 [0.002, 0.08]. Round 1's pooled control was biased downward. Newcomers start at their lab's field ($a(1)=0.21$, $p=0.005$) and drift away. At the read-out call, a message pulls a \textbf{same-lab reader's content 1.7$\times$ more} (0.067 vs 0.039; 8/8 units). About half of that is lab-level susceptibility (post hoc). Talk shows no family term.}

\hmodel{Model 11 (vector spins). $v_{i,d}$ is agent $i$'s mean whitened chat embedding on day $d$, and $m_d$ is the day mean. $h_f$ is the field of lab $f(i)$, $a_i$ an agent offset, and $H_i$ the agent field. Style rival: each statement vector $z$ is regressed on its style features $s$ (formatting, function words, genre held out) with a map $B$ fitted within agents (W) or pooled (P). Coupling: $J_1$ is the matched-age jump in $\cos(z_B,z_m)$ between the recipient's read-out call ($h=1$) and the call in flight ($h=0$), for same-lab and cross-lab pairs.}

\hmath{\vspace{-8pt}\begin{gather*}
v_{i,d}=m_d+h_{f(i)}+a_i+\varepsilon_{i,d},\qquad T=\langle\cos H_iH_j\rangle_{\rm same}-\langle\cos H_iH_j\rangle_{\rm cross}\\

\end{gather*}\vspace{-10pt}}

\htelos{Vendors differ mostly in prose, and partly in what they say. A pooled style correction removes the prose and then pushes the vendor field below zero. A within-agent correction leaves about a third of it. Vendors also differ in how strongly their models are moved by what they read. A same-vendor message moves a reader 1.7$\times$ as much as a cross-vendor one, at the next call. Half of that is because some vendors' models are more easily moved, not in-group preference. Who replies does not depend on vendor. New models arrive sounding like their vendor and lose part of it within a week. For an operator: expect a read message to move a same-vendor model up to 1.7$\times$ as much as another vendor's model.}

\hbottom{Model families carry a content field that is mostly style, and content couples more within labs at the read-out call; talk does not.}
