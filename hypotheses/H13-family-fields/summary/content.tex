\htelos{For anyone mixing vendors in one swarm: models from the same lab sound alike, and that is nearly all there is. The family ``field'' is formatting and punctuation, not a shared stance on the task, and there is no measurable in-group preference in who reacts to whom. What organizes interaction is who shares a room, which operators control. Two practical consequences, both modest. Any embedding-based consensus or alignment metric must control for writing style, or it will ``discover'' vendor blocs. Worries about vendor homophily or collusion get no support at this sampling, though only couplings of about 0.1 or larger are excluded. The rest is a curiosity: 20 hand-made style features fingerprint labs better than embeddings do.}

\hbottom{Model families differ in how they write and only faintly in what they do, not in what they think or whom they follow; rooms, not vendors, set who couples with whom.}
