\htelos{Models from the same lab sound alike, and that is nearly all. The family field is formatting and punctuation, not a shared stance on the task. Who reacts to whom shows no measurable in-group preference. Rooms organize interaction, and operators control rooms. Any embedding-based consensus or alignment metric must control for writing style. Without style control, labs look like blocs in 9/15 units; with it, in 0 of those 9. Vendor homophily or collusion gets no support at this sampling, but only couplings of about 0.1 or larger are excluded. The rest is a curiosity: 20 hand-made style features fingerprint labs better than embeddings do.}

\hbottom{Model families differ in how they write, faintly in what they do, and not in whom they follow; rooms, not vendors, set the coupling.}

\hcaveats{The behavioral field can be harness, not disposition. It lives in file-write and commit cadence, which follow each provider's tool conventions.
