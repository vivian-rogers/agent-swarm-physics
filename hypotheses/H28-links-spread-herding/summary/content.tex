\hwhat{In 14 non-holdout goal periods we asked whether an agent is more likely to switch to project X soon after seeing a chat link to X: a 5-min hazard model with agent, project and day effects. Seeing a link goes with a $2.3\times$ switch hazard (pooled; $p<0.05$ in 13/14). But it beats links shifted by $\pm$30--120 min in only 3/11 herding weeks (primary test failed). Future links predict switches better than past ones in 10/11, and naive agents already switch at $3.3\times$ baseline in the hour before their first exposure. Links lead visits only in own-artifact weeks, where nobody herds. Simple beats complex contagion. NE09 failed. Synthetic swarms: 0--4\% false positives, 92--100\% power. Holdout script written, not run.}

\hmodel{Multi-label kinetic Potts (model 10) with a contagion field (model 03). An off-project agent $i$ switches into $X$ at rate $\mu_{iX}$. $\alpha_i,\pi_X,\delta_d$: agent, project and day fields. $E_{iX}$: a link to $X$ by another agent became visible to $i$ in the last 60 min. $\kappa$: link coupling. $n_{-i,X}$: others on $X$ (Potts coupling $J$). $\mathbf h$: own history, plans, pulses. $\lambda$: extra switches per exposure. $R_{\rm link}$: share of switches attributable to links. $\pi$: links per switch. $k_s$: susceptible recipients per link.}

\hmath{\vspace{-5pt}\begin{gather*}
\log\mu_{iX}(t)=\alpha_i+\pi_X+\delta_d+\kappa\,E_{iX}(t)+J\log\!\big(1+n_{-i,X}(t)\big)+\boldsymbol\eta\!\cdot\!\mathbf h_{iX}(t)\\
\lambda=\frac{\sum(\hat\mu-\hat\mu|_{E=0})}{N_{\rm exp}},\qquad R_{\rm link}=\pi\,k_s\,\lambda=\frac{\sum(\hat\mu-\hat\mu|_{E=0})}{\sum y}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-7pt}}

\htelos{The operator question was: would throttling link-sharing damp pile-ons? Mostly no. In herding weeks links, chat and work on a project rise together. The link marks an attention burst already under way more than it starts one. Removing every link trims simulated peak pile-ons by about a fifth at most; halving them changes little. The usable outputs are diagnostics. The lead/lag test tells an operator whether links act as directed recruitment (lag $\gg$ lead: agents advertising their own artifacts, $\lambda\approx0.01$--$0.02$ per exposure) or as markers (lead $\ge$ lag). The bounds are $\lambda\lesssim0.1$ and $R_{\rm link}\lesssim0.45$. To prevent duplicated work, target ownership or the burst triggers, not links.}

\hbottom{Links co-move with project switches (hazard ratio $\approx2.3$) but do not lead them in herding weeks, so they mark attention bursts more than spread them; throttling them would trim pile-ons by at most $\sim$20\%.}
