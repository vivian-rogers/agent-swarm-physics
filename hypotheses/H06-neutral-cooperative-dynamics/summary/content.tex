\hwhat{Each agent slot carries one project label per 30-min window. The label is a cluster of the agent's self-written goal, the repo it links, or (round 1b) the repo it commits to. Exact finite-$N$ simulations with the real label gaps fit three models to 9 statistics: neutral cooperative dynamics (NCD), Hubbell drift and a herding rival. \textbf{NCD fails} in free weeks (4/5 failed, 1 mixed). In rounds 1 and 1b the joint check rejects all three models on stated goals in every free week ($p<0.01$). In free weeks 72--91\% of goal clusters have a single agent. A second embedding model and style removal (round 1b) leave this intact. \#35 is a control week with a known project: one fork per room. Its stated goals are just as fragmented, but 96--100\% of work sits on the room's fork. Goal clusters measure sub-tasks, not projects. Work labels concentrate on a shared repo where one exists. There Hubbell-like copying can fit (\#30), without NCD's signature. Copying follows reading: a project named in a read message predicts a switch (OR 8.6 against unread).}

\hmodel{Model 06 (Pi\~nero et al.\ 2025). $N$ individuals (agent slots) each hold one species (project). In each step, with probability $\mu$, a new project replaces a random slot $C$. Otherwise $C$ copies the project of slot $A$ only if $A$ and a third slot $B$ hold different projects. This is cooperation: rare projects recruit more per capita. $\lambda=\sum_i x_i^2$ is the Simpson index of the shares $x_i=n_i/N$. The rivals are Hubbell (always copy) and herding (copy favoured when $A$ and $B$ agree).}

\hmath{\vspace{-8pt}\begin{gather*}
b_n=(1-\mu)\tfrac nN\big(1-\tfrac nN\big)^2,\qquad
P_n\propto\frac{(1-\mu)^n}{n}\,e^{-(n-N\lambda^*)^2/2N}\\
\tfrac1\mu=\sqrt{\tfrac{\pi N}{2}}\,\mathrm{erfcx}\!\Big(\sqrt{\tfrac N2}\,(\mu-\lambda^*)\Big),\quad
\mu_B=\tfrac{e^{-2}}{\sqrt{2\pi N}},\ \ \mu_L=\sqrt{\tfrac{2}{\pi N}}\\
P(\text{recruit to } i)\propto x_i\,e^{\beta x_i}:\ \ \beta<0\ \text{NCD},\ 0\ \text{Hubbell},\ >0\ \text{herding}
\end{gather*}\vspace{-10pt}}

\htelos{Neutral cooperative dynamics does not describe these swarms. What agents \emph{say} they do is a spread of private sub-tasks in every week, so stated-goal abundances are a poor project census. What agents \emph{work} on concentrates on a shared repo when the task has one. It spreads out when the task gives each agent its own artifact or role (\#44 free room, \#51), as H11 finds for effort. Copying runs through reading: a read mention raises the switch odds 8.6-fold over an unread one. To get a shared project, assign it or announce it in a message the agents read. To monitor, compute the work-label singleton fraction and the copy-consistency.}

\hbottom{Free LLM agents state private sub-tasks but work on shared repos when one exists; NCD fails, neutral copying fits at best, and copying follows reading.}
