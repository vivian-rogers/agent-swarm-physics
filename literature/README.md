# Literature index

Seven papers on information, replicators, thermodynamics and agency. Each has a notes file with the same structure (summary, formalism, mapping to the AI Village data, hypotheses, caveats). Dataset schema: `../data/raw/ai-village/SCHEMA.md`; scaffolding history: `../data/raw/ai-village/CHANGELOG.md`.

| Paper | Year | Fields | One-line takeaway |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Kolchinsky & Wolpert, Semantic information, autonomous agency and non-equilibrium statistical physics](kolchinsky-2018-semantic-information-autonomous-agency.md) | 2018 | info theory, thermodynamics, stat mech | Semantic information is the part of agent-environment mutual information (or transfer entropy) that, when scrambled, reduces the agent's viability. |
| [Sowinski et al., Semantic Information in a Model of Resource Gathering Agents](sowinski-2023-semantic-information-resource-gathering-agents.md) | 2023 | info theory, dynamics | Foragers show a viability plateau then collapse as sensor information is scrambled; the semantic threshold depends only on sensor and body geometry. |
| [Kolchinsky, Thermodynamic dissipation does not bound replicator growth and decay rates](kolchinsky-2024-dissipation-does-not-bound-replicator-rates.md) | 2024 | thermodynamics, stat mech, dynamics | England's dissipation bound cannot hold universally: first-order growth plus first-order decay into reactants is thermodynamically inconsistent, and decay into waste decouples the two. |
| [Piñero et al., Neutral theory of cooperative dynamics](pinero-2025-neutral-theory-cooperative-dynamics.md) | 2025 | stat mech, dynamics, sociophysics | Requiring cross-species cooperation to replicate yields a bimodal abundance distribution with a long-lived cooperator core at low migration. |
| [Bartlett et al., Physics of Life: Exploring Information as a Distinctive Feature of Living Systems](bartlett-2025-physics-of-life-information-roadmap.md) | 2025 | info theory, dynamics, thermodynamics | Roadmap arguing that using information for viability is measurable (semantic thresholds, fitness value of information, agnostic biosignatures), with coarse-graining and open-endedness as open problems. |
| [Piñero et al., Information bounds production in replicator systems](pinero-2026-information-bounds-replicator-production.md) | 2026 | info theory, dynamics, stat mech | Replicator productivity in fluctuating environments splits into environmental uncertainty, side-information benefit and strategy mismatch, with a Kelly-like optimal strategy. |
| [Aguilera, Ito & Kolchinsky, Inferring Entropy Production in Many-Body Systems Using Nonequilibrium Maximum Entropy](aguilera-2026-entropy-production-nonequilibrium-maxent.md) | 2026 | stat mech, thermodynamics, info theory | A convex dual of a nonequilibrium MaxEnt problem lower-bounds entropy production from trajectory correlations alone, scaling to 1000 units and recovering asymmetric couplings. |

## Cross-cutting themes

- **Shared lineage and vocabulary.** Kolchinsky is an author of all seven papers, Piñero of three (including the two replicator papers and Sowinski 2023), and Frank and Ghoshal of three. All use viability, replicators, side information and Kelly-style growth, so measurements defined for one paper (e.g., transfer entropy to an agent's actions) transfer to the others.
- **Two strands plus a measuring tool.** (a) Semantic or functional information: Kolchinsky & Wolpert 2018, then Sowinski 2023, Bartlett 2025 and Piñero 2026. (b) Replicator and population dynamics: Kolchinsky 2024, Piñero 2025 and 2026. (c) Aguilera 2026 supplies an estimator (entropy production, directed couplings) that applies to any binned activity data.
- **Counterfactual interventions versus observational logs.** The semantic-information papers need scrambling interventions, which the static AI Village export lacks. Substitutes are the natural experiments listed in `CHANGELOG.md` (rooms v1 on 2026-02-25, perma-computer-use on 2026-03-24, the auto-nudger, the 200-event context cap on 2026-06-11, village hours change on 2026-06-29) and offline replay of logged contexts with permuted memory or chat.
- **Thermodynamic caution.** Kolchinsky 2024 shows dissipation need not bound growth/decay, and Aguilera 2026 treats entropy production as statistical irreversibility, not heat. For a swarm, "thermodynamic" quantities must be defined operationally (token or dollar cost, time-reversal asymmetry of activity) and no universal bound should be assumed.
- **Roster as migration and competition.** New models joining (`CHANGELOG.md` roster) play the role of migration in Piñero 2025 and of changing environments favoring different "winners" in Piñero 2026. Village goals (`village_goals`) and rest or work periods map to its active and inactive phases and environmental states, but with only ~46 goals statistical power is limited.
- **Gaps and recurring risk.** None of the seven is an epidemic or contagion model. The birth-death master equation (Kolchinsky 2024) and the discussion of higher-order contagion (Piñero 2025) are the closest links, so the contagion literature on meme or convention spread among agents still needs to be added. Every method depends on a coarse-graining of text into discrete states (topics, action classes), so a shared, documented preprocessing pipeline and sensitivity checks are needed before comparing results across papers.
