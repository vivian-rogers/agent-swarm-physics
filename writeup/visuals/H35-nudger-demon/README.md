# H35 visual: the nudger as an inefficient Maxwell demon (H60 companion)

`make.py` builds `fig.pdf`/`fig.png` (double column; static only — the result is an efficiency and a policy ranking, not a dynamic). Inputs: `data/processed/H35-nudger-maxwell-demon/G51/results.json` (`minute_k_eff`: frontier, ceiling half-range, logged bits and ΔV with bootstrap CI, η_SU, trap-age response and nudge shares), `G51/r1b/results_r1b.json` (glance η_SU) and `G51/r1b/results_r1b_did.json` (work-commit η_SU after the post hoc DiD), and `data/processed/H60-index-nudge-policy/G51/results.json` (`calls30` policy values and CIs). The k = 2–3 and k ≥ 10 policy points in (a) are recomputed with H35's own `policy_info`/`policy_value` formulas (copied verbatim, not imported, to avoid importing h16lib) on the stored shrunk response. Caption numbers are from the H35 card (round-1 "Outcome vs prediction" P4/P5 and Reading 3–4; round-1b table) and the H60 card headline and G51 row. Note the two "once-early" rules differ: H35's is a nudge at re-pause k = 2–3 on TS2r chains (minute level); H60's is the second idle gate of a trap (glance-robust clock). #51 days are non-holdout (08-20 nudger stop and the held-out tail are outside the inputs); no text is read.
