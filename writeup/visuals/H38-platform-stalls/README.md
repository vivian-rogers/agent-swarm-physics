# H38: platform stalls are scheduled

`fig.pdf` (double column). Panel (a) is a simulation of the H25/H38 estimator: ±1 minute spins, each agent's mean removed in 30-min blocks, g = 1 − 1/VR. Agents start and stop near the operator's resume and pause, and inside the day they follow a weakly coupled kinetic Ising update. The gain is computed on whole days and on the all-present window. Panel (b) uses H38's round-1b `r1b/period_table.parquet` for all 35 non-holdout periods. Bar height is `E_raw` (excess over the N1 block-shift null), split into `E_mask_scaffold` (blue, what survives scaffold conditioning) and `E_raw − E_mask_scaffold` (orange). The gray bars are ±1.96 × the null SD (`E_raw / z_raw`). A black tick marks the raw gain where conditioning raised the estimate (e.g. #44). Card numbers: f_scaffold 0.72 (regime III, 8 periods) and 0.11 (regime I, periods with z_raw > 2, H38's own significance rule); f_trim 0.83 (Round 1b table). The animation (`anim.mp4`, 20 s, simulation) scrolls three simulated days of the raster and the active count. Stacked bars update live: whole-day gain = inside-window co-activation (blue) + start/stop synchrony (orange). It ends with a fade. Build: `uv run python writeup/visuals/H38-platform-stalls/make.py [--fig|--anim]`.
