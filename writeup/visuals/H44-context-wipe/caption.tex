\caption{A forced context wipe costs output and starts a short re-reading phase.
(a) Schematic: the scaffold wipes the context window at the 41-record cap (40 calls); the agent then re-reads.
(b)--(d) Measured in G51 (14{,}593 forced wipes), calls aligned to the wipe (vermillion, 95\% agent-day bootstrap band) and to a no-reset boundary at call 21 of a segment (gray band, the null).
At the first call after a wipe the re-acquisition share rises from 0.22 to 0.47 and relaxes over about 5--7 calls; writes and work commits dip and recover over about 3 calls.
(e) Work commits over calls $+1\ldots+10$ relative to calls $-20\ldots-11$, per period: pooled $-38\%$ $[-41,-35]$ after forced wipes (H15's ledger estimator: $-39\%$ $[-42,-35]$, 7/9 periods), against a no-reset control that rises because output ramps up through every segment.
(f) A command loop in the last 10 calls recurs in the next 10 calls far less often after a wipe (G38: 16\% vs 72\%; pooled OR 0.11 $[0.05, 0.26]$, 7 periods; bars are binomial 95\% intervals).}
