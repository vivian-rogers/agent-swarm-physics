\caption{\textbf{Village content changes by transmission; selection is not detectable (H89).}
(a)~The cultural Price split of the day-to-day change of the mean trait (schematic): stayers change (transmission), stayers whose ideas others adopt inside the logged light cone have higher fitness $w$ (selection), and leavers and entrants move the mean (migration).
(b)~Cross-fitted energy share of transmission in the change of mean content (bge statement vectors) for the 33 eligible periods, with agent-jackknife 95\% intervals; dashes, the second embedding model (gte). Median 0.98 in both models; $\ge0.5$ and the largest term in 33 of 33 periods.
Lower (b): the small terms, selection (median $|s_\mathrm{Sel}|=0.010$; beyond its fitness-permutation null in 1 of 33 periods, circled, against 1.7 expected by chance), migration (median 0.004; visible only in the largest, most open periods \#38, \#40, \#51), and the part of transmission along the kickoff direction (orange, the exogenous field; median 0.04). The synthetic validation shows that selection in a vector trait cannot be detected at village counts, so its small share is a bound set by the design, not a discovery.
(c)~Migration share of style (20 text features) against content: style is above content in 21 of 26 periods with any turnover (open circle: 7 periods with no entries or exits).}
