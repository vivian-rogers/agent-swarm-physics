# H97 visual: a kickoff is a restoring force

`make.py` builds `fig.pdf`/`fig.png` (double column) and `anim.mp4` + `anim_poster.png` (1280×720, 24 fps, 22.9 s; `--no-anim` / `--anim-only`). Panel (a) and the animation show one real kickoff, #38 → #39 (H97 design `T39`: regime III, 13 agents on both sides, the largest Δρ, +0.60), projected by the shared pipeline `writeup/visuals/_quench_common.py`: x = ⟨z, k̂₃₉⟩ (H97's whitened kickoff vector, bge), y = ⟨z, ê₃₈⟩ (the #38 last-day centroid orthogonalized to k̂). The animation plays active time only (2-h exponential kernel per agent, τ = 40 min; nights are short fades; the first 90 min after the kickoff run at half speed) and shows the snap, the day-1 overshoot past the days 2–5 level, and the settle; the bars give this kickoff's ρ = 0.23 against its ordinary-night ρ₀ = 0.84 (`G39/results.json`). Panel (b) is an event study: ρ_full on consecutive active-day boundaries at offsets −4…+3 around the 18 primary kickoffs, with the same estimator as the card (`h97lib.memory_corr`, split halves of both days, 20 splits); offset 0 is the stored kickoff ρ, the gray band is the card's 161 placebo boundaries (`NE34/placebo_gaps.parquet`). The other offsets are a light recomputation for this figure (not in the card); they sit at 0.64–0.67, inside the placebo band. Panel (c) and all caption numbers come from `NE34/summary.json` (card Status and "Outcome vs prediction": P1, P2, P3 intercept, centered variant). Inputs: `data/processed/H97-quench-restoring-force/` and the shared DQ5 statement vectors; non-holdout only (asserted with `holdout_mask`); no text is read.
