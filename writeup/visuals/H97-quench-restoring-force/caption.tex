Gray arrows: an ordinary night inside \#38; orange arrows: the kickoff. The day-1 mean (diamond) lies beyond the settled level of days 2--5 (dashed).
(b)~Cross-agent memory $\rho$ (disattenuated correlation of agent positions before and after a day boundary) around 18 kickoffs: median and bootstrap 90\% CI (black; thin lines, single kickoffs); gray, the 161 ordinary-night placebo boundaries (dark: IQR, light: 5--95\%).
