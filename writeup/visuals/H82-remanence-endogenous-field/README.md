# H82 visual: remanence that does not fade

`make.py` builds `fig.pdf`/`fig.png` (double column, static). Inputs: `data/processed/H82-remanence-endogenous-field/replication/boundary_rows.parquet` (config `primary`, term `e`: per-boundary Δγ_d, γ_prev, γ_next, γ_placebo; the figure shows pooled means over boundaries, the card's decision statistic after Amendment A1, with a boundary-bootstrap 95% CI computed here), `replication/replication.json` (S0 95th percentiles), and `posthoc/posthoc.json` (PH2, means only; no CI is stored, so panel (c) has none). The green curves are the HH's decaying-remanence form Δγ₁ e^{−(d−1)/τ_R}, anchored at the bge day-1 value, at the edges of the card's P3 range. Caption numbers are from the card Status, "Outcome vs prediction" (P1, P2, P3) and "Post hoc checks" (PH2). Non-holdout only; no text is read.
