# H34: idea cascades

`fig.pdf` (double column) uses H34's round-1b (context-ledger visibility) outputs. Panel (a) is the CCDF of exposure-tree sizes from `r1b/G51/trees.parquet` (hashed markers, no text), with Wilson 95% intervals. The gray band is a parametric envelope: 2,000 multinomial draws of 52,874 trees from H34's own truncated GW-NB law (`h34stats.nb_gw_pmf_trunc`, R̂ and k̂ from `r1b/results/period_table.parquet`, cls ALL). The thin lines are the other 31 periods. Panel (b) plots R̂ with its CI for the 32 periods against `N_room`; regime labels come from H67's period table. Card numbers: R̂ 0.09–0.40, median 0.22, upper CI < 1 in 32/32 (Round 1b table); size-5+ trees heavier than one branching law (Results §3). The 0.23 shown for #51 is the round-1b value; round 1's was 0.221. The animation (`writeup/animations/H34-idea-cascades.mp4`, 20 s, simulation) grows 24 idea trees side by side at R = 0.2 (village) and R = 1 (critical). Offspring are NB with k = 1.3 (the operator table's dispersion), trees are capped at a 25-agent room, and the real #51 CCDF sits underneath. The movie fades to empty and restarts. Build: `uv run python writeup/visuals/H34-idea-cascades/make.py [--fig|--anim]`.
