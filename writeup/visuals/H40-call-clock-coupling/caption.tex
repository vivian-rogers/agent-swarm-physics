\caption{\textbf{In the computer-use scaffold agents couple per model call, not per minute.}
(a)~Analytic illustration: two agents with the same reply hazard per call (0.08), one calling every 8\,s and one every 40\,s. Their reply curves coincide when counted in calls (collapse) and fan out in minutes (smear).
(b)~The exposure-time elasticity $\eta$ (the coefficient of the log wall time a call spans in a cloglog reply-hazard model) in 33 non-holdout periods, with 95\% CIs. The horizontal lines and bands are random-effects means by regime: I $+0.51$ [0.41, 0.61], II $-0.01$ [$-0.12$, 0.11], III $-0.16$ [$-0.32$, 0.01]. The call clock is $\eta=0$ and the wall clock $\eta=1$. G38 and G44 lie below zero, which neither clock explains.
(c,\,d)~G51 heavy spins, 243 agent-units (marker area $\propto$ weight, within-unit centred). The per-call coupling is flat in call rate ($s=+0.11$ [$-0.03$, 0.25]), and the wall-clock rival $s=-1$ (dashed) is excluded. The per-hour coupling rises as $1+s=1.11$ [0.97, 1.25] (model-free 1.00 [0.81, 1.19]), where the wall clock predicts no slope.}
\label{fig:H40}
