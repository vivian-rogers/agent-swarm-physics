# H16 + H72: trap aging

`fig.pdf`/`fig.png` (double column): (a) a simulation of per-gate escape probability vs trap age for a single trap depth (Kramers, memoryless) and a broad depth distribution (Bouchaud-type aging, μ = 0.5), each with one kick at odds ×1.54; (b) the G51 idle-gate table from H72 (`gates.parquet`, goal 51, holdout masked with `holdout_mask`, 22,797 gates with a trap age and an outcome = the card's n): binned share of gates escaping into a sustained run by trap age `a_sus`, split by a directed read at the gate (`n_dir > 0`: mention or own nudge), agent-day bootstrap bands (200 draws), and a null band from within-agent permutation of trap age; (c) H72's FE-logit aging slopes without and with the starvation clock and the input-clock slope for G17, G18, G38, G51. `writeup/animations/H16-trap-aging.mp4` (13.0 s, 1280×720): a schematic particle in a well that deepens with age; the hazard panel uses the G51 TS1r deep slope (−0.77, H16) as its exponent and shows one directed kick (odds ×1.54) letting the particle out, then a fresh shallow trap.

Panel (b) is descriptive (raw binned shares, no agent effects); the slopes in the caption come from the cards. Numbers: H72 card "Results by goal period" and "Outcome vs prediction" (β_a0, β_a, β_s per period); H16 card round 1 table (gate OR for directed kicks 1.5–2.9, G51 1.54; TS1r deep β −0.77) and round 1b (NE43: aging unchanged with the nudger off).
