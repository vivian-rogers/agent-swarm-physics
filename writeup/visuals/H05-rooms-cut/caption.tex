(a)~Schematic of the two-block kinetic Ising picture: room-mates read each other ($J_\mathrm{in}$), and the cut removes the cross-room reads ($J_\mathrm{out}\approx0$). (b)~Within-room minus cross-room excess lagged coupling $\kappa_x$ (over the cross-day surrogate) in the six regime-III two-room windows; gray: room-label null, $\pm1.96$~sd. Talk coupling follows the room in 6/6 windows (Fisher $p=6\times10^{-7}$); activity spins, the actual work, do not ($p=0.17$). (c)~At the 05-11 split (NE42, not held out) the 40 cut pairs lose talk coupling relative to the 51 pairs that stay together (DiD $-0.020$, assignment-permutation $p=0.027$; gray: permutation null, 95\%). Pooled over regime-III pair-days, co-location raises $\kappa_x$ by 0.012 ($z=5.1$); with ledger read counts in the model it drops to 0.001 ($z=0.2$) while reads keep $z=2.1$, and activity spins show no co-location effect. On the holdout, the 03-16 split (NE15) gave a pair DiD of $-0.022$ [$-0.038$, $-0.008$] ($p=0.021$), but the pre-registered verdict is inconclusive.}
