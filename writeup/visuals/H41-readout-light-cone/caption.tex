\caption{\textbf{A logged light cone for novel items: it binds inside a room, and across rooms it is a cage.}
(a)~One novel item (a number) first posted by DeepSeek-V3.2 in G38's \#rest (2026-04-06), drawn on the ledger's model calls. Blue shading is each agent's logged light cone: the time after its first call that read a message on a time-respecting read-out path from the post. All seven room-mates enter the cone at their next call (within 2.6\,min), and two adopt the item inside it. No \#best agent enters the cone that day, yet all four adopt the item 60--72\,min later, outside it ($\times$). Three later \#rest adoptions (about 21\,h later) are inside the cone and lie off-axis.
(b)~Per period, the share of adoptions outside the logged cone (robust to call-start uncertainty). Adopters in the source's room: median 0.6\%, maximum 4\%, over 32 non-holdout periods. Adopters in another room: 75--100\% in 7/8 two-room periods (G44: 11\%). In G51, where agents hop rooms, it is 47\%.}
