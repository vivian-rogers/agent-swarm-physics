\caption{\textbf{Collective memory of a finished goal period decays fast to a small floor, and newcomers forget at the veterans' pace (H88).}
(a)~Veterans' attention share to a finished period's coined terms against village days since the period ended, each period normalized by its own fitted share at day~1 (thin gray: per-period exponential-plus-floor fits; black: median over 20 periods in day bins, IQR bars). Curves at the median fitted parameters: exponential plus floor ($\tau=5.5$ village days, floor 1.9\% of the initial share; best model in 8 of 20 periods), biexponential where it won ($\tau_1=2.0$, $\tau_2=48$\,d; 4/20) and a single exponential (5/20).
(b)~Newcomers' standardized attention relative to veterans' at the same age of the terms (indirect standardization, period-bootstrap 95\% CI): 0.93, 0.64, 0.79 by age band, with no rise ($b=-0.17\,[-0.80,+1.32]$). Dashed gray: equal decay; green: the Candia two-compartment prediction that newcomers carry only the slow component (synthetic, $b=+0.79$; power 0.87--0.92).}
