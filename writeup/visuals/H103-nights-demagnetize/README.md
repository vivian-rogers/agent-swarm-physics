# H103 visual: nights do not demagnetize

`make.py` builds `fig.pdf`/`fig.png` (double column, static; `--period G10` picks the example period). Panel (a) is a labelled simulation of the two clocks. Panel (b) refits #10's stored O1 series (`data/processed/H103-nights-demagnetize/results/o1_bge_small_style_resid.json`, `series`: H, N, A, n, slot) with a copy of `h103lib.fit_clock` (grid-profiled weighted LS with slot effects); the SSE ratio reproduces the card's 0.71 (#10's winner is H). #10 was chosen as a clear example of the within-day decline and overnight partial recovery; it is not typical in size (D = −0.117). Panel (c) uses `results/card_{bge_small,gte_modernbert}_style_resid.json` (O3 β_N random-effects means) and `synthetic/synthetic.json` (O3 rows under active-hour, night and wall-clock truths). Caption numbers are from the card Status and "Outcome vs prediction" (P1, P3a). Non-holdout only; no text is read.
