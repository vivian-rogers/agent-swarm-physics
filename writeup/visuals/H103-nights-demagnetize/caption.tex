\caption{\textbf{Nights do not demagnetize: kickoff remanence decays with active work, not per night (H103).}
(a)~Simulation of the two candidate clocks: a night clock (one multiplicative step per night, flat during the day) and an active-hour clock (decay during work, flat over the night); nights drawn short.
(b)~Kickoff remanence $A_w$ in 30-min windows of goal period \#10 (bge, style-residualized; symbol size, agents in the window) against active hours since the kickoff, with H103's night-clock (green) and active-hour (orange) fits, both with time-of-day terms and equal parameter counts; dotted lines mark nights. The alignment falls inside each day and partly recovers overnight. Across the 22 periods with decay the active-hour clock fits as well or better in 17/22 (bge) and 16/22 (gte); the night clock wins in 5/22 / 3/22 (synthetic clock-selection accuracy 0.82).
(c)~Night step at fixed active lag $\beta_N$: village random-effects mean over 41 period units, $-0.001\,[-0.023,+0.021]$ (bge) and $-0.002\,[-0.028,+0.024]$ (gte), against H103's synthetic truths (median, 10--90\% of 20 replicates): active-hour clock $\approx0$, night clock $-0.17$, wall clock $-0.22$.}
