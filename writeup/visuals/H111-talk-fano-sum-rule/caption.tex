\caption{A fluctuation--response sum rule for talk. (a) Simulation: in a linear Hawkes talk swarm (15 agents, one room, 1-min read-out kernel) the collective Fano ratio of 10-min talk counts, $\Phi=\mathrm{Var}(\sum_i n_i)/\sum_i\mathrm{Var}(n_i)$, follows the room-adjusted prediction $\Phi_{pred}(g)\approx1/(1-g)^2$ with no free parameter; the gray band is the no-coupling spread. The estimator reads slightly low at large $g$ because cascades straddle window edges, which is small in the village range ($g_{lag}\le0.29$). (b) Village: observed $\Phi$(10 min) per unit against $\Phi_{pred}$ built from H67's $g_{lag}$ (95\% CIs both ways; 41 units with $\ge40$ usable windows). Regime III closes, pooled $r_F=\Phi_{obs}/\Phi_{pred}=1.01$ [0.94, 1.09] over 18 units; regime I carries an unexplained fast excess, $r_F=1.34$ [1.18, 1.52]; gray shading marks values below the independent-agent (block-shift) null's 95th percentile.}
