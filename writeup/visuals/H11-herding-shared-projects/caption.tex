\caption{\textbf{Agents herd onto shared projects, in attention and in work commits (H11).}
(a)~Curie--Weiss Potts cartoon: projects are wells and agents are balls; $\beta J<0$ spreads agents over projects, $\beta J>0$ piles them into one.
(b)~Goal period \#31 (free week): agents on the busiest project per 30-min window, from strict artifact mentions (blue) and from agent work commits (red, dashed); gray band, 95\% interval of the circular-shift null N2, which keeps each agent's own persistence; up to 11 agents mention one repo in one window and 9 commit to one.
(c)~Same-project co-location share minus its N2 mean, per unit from \#30 on (gray bars, null $\pm1.96$\,SD); the excess is about equal in attention and work (\#31: $+0.07$ in both).
(d)~Coupling beyond agent fields, $z_{N2}$, per period, sorted by artifact ownership; gray band, $|z|<2$. Attention herds ($\beta J_\mathrm{CW}>0$) in 11 of 14 tested weeks whatever the goal mode, with $z_{N2}\ge2$ in 10 of 11 shared-artifact weeks; work herds ($z_{N2}\ge2$) in 4 of 6 testable shared weeks (open square: \#37, too few work blocks). The exceptions are own-artifact weeks (spread by agent fields, not avoidance) and \#35 ($z_{N2}=-2.5$, a shared week against the pattern).}
