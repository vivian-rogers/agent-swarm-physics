# H54 visual: the kickoff text is the quench target

`make.py` builds `fig.pdf`/`fig.png` (double column, static; no animation: the result is an identification matrix, not a motion). Inputs: `data/processed/H54-kickoff-quench-target/NE34/S_kick.npy` (33 × 33 day-1 centroid × kickoff target scores, bge), `NE34/periods.parquet` (ranks; the script recomputes the genericness-corrected ranks and asserts they equal the card's), `NE34/results.json` (P1: median π, top-1, Wilcoxon p), `G51/native.json` (role-swap accuracy overall and weekly), and the shared `period_affordances` (mode F marks free weeks). Caption numbers are from the card Status, "Results by goal period" and "Outcome vs prediction" (P1, N1). The weekly CIs are binomial on pairs (pairs share agents, so they are narrower than a permutation interval). Non-holdout only (H54's scheme drops held-out periods); no text is read.
