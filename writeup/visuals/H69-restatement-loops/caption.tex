The gray null puts a fake boundary at each segment's midpoint. (c) A forced erasure between two statements raises the odds that a loop ends 2.40$\times$ $[1.18, 4.88]$ and lowers the odds that one starts to 0.54$\times$ $[0.34, 0.86]$ (logits with agent effects, agent-day cluster errors; random-effects pools).}
