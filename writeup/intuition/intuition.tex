\begin{abstract}
Short notes on the 15 hypotheses with the highest estimated usefulness (EU = credence $\times$ value if true, scored by two Claude judges, one blind). Each note gives the question, the physical intuition, the measurement, the result, the consequence for an operator, and the caveats, with the writeup figure. Animations, where they exist, are in \texttt{writeup/visuals/<folder>/anim.mp4}. No claim has passed a confirmation test on reserved data. Companion to the paper \texttt{writeup/paper/paper.pdf} and the hypothesis compendium.
\end{abstract}
